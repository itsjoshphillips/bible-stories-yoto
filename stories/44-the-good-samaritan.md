# Fidelity notes
# - Keeps the frame: the parable answers the lawyer's question "Who is my
#   neighbour?", and Jesus's closing question and command are his own.
# - Not softened: the robbers beat the man and leave him half dead (10:30).
# - Explanatory gloss, flagged for parental review: "The people of Israel and
#   the Samaritans did not like each other at all" (see John 4:9, Luke 9:52-53).
#   Without it the point of choosing a Samaritan is lost on a child.
# - The priest and the Levite are called a priest and "a helper from God's
#   temple". The text gives no reason why they passed by, and none is invented.
# - "Two silver coins" renders two denarii (10:35).
# - Omitted: the lawyer's motives ("to test him", "to justify himself").

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The Good Samaritan.

NARRATOR: One day, a man who knew God's law very well stood up to ask Jesus a question.

LAWYER: Teacher, what must I do to live for ever with God?

JESUS: What is written in God's law?

LAWYER: Love the Lord your God with all your heart. And love your neighbour as you love yourself.

JESUS: That is right. Do this, and you will live.

LAWYER: But who is my neighbour?

NARRATOR: So Jesus told him a story.

[SCENE]

JESUS: A man was going down the road from Jerusalem to Jericho. And robbers jumped on him.

NARRATOR: The robbers took his clothes. They beat him. Then they went away, and left him lying by the road, half dead.

NARRATOR: Now, a priest came down that road. He saw the hurt man. And he walked past, on the other side.

NARRATOR: Next came a helper from God's temple. He saw the hurt man too. And he walked past, on the other side.

NARRATOR: Then along came a Samaritan. Now, the people of Israel and the Samaritans did not like each other at all. But when this Samaritan saw the hurt man, his heart was full of kindness.

NARRATOR: He went to him. He poured oil and wine on his sore places, and wrapped them up. He lifted the man onto his own donkey. He took him to an inn, and looked after him.

NARRATOR: The next day, the Samaritan gave two silver coins to the innkeeper.

SAMARITAN: [kindly] Take care of him. And if you spend more than this, I will pay you back when I return.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: That was the story. Then Jesus asked the man a question.

JESUS: Which of these three was a neighbour to the man who was hurt?

LAWYER: The one who was kind to him.

JESUS: [warmly] Go, and do the same.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Luke, chapter ten.
