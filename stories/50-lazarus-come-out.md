# Fidelity notes
# - Not softened: Lazarus is dead, four days in the tomb, and Jesus says so
#   plainly (11:14). Jesus's tears (11:35) are kept.
# - Every spoken line is a close paraphrase of John's wording. Martha's
#   "there will be a bad smell" is in the text (11:39).
# - Mary's words (11:32) are the same as Martha's and are reported by the
#   narrator, not given a separate voice.
# - Omitted: the disciples' fear of returning to Judea and Thomas's remark
#   (11:8-16), "This illness is for the glory of God" (11:4), Jesus's prayer
#   of thanks at the tomb (11:41-42), the mourners' comments (11:36-37).
# - "Jesus waited two more days" is stated without a reason, as in John.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Lazarus, Come Out!

NARRATOR: Jesus had three dear friends. Their names were Martha, and Mary, and their brother, Lazarus. Jesus loved them.

NARRATOR: One day, Lazarus became very ill. His sisters sent a message to Jesus. It said: "Lord, the one you love is ill."

NARRATOR: But Jesus did not go straight away. He stayed where he was for two more days. Then he said to his disciples,

JESUS: Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep. I am going to wake him up.

DISCIPLE: Lord, if he is asleep, he will get better.

NARRATOR: But Jesus did not mean that kind of sleep. So he told them plainly.

JESUS: Lazarus has died.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: When Jesus came to the village, Lazarus had been in his tomb for four days. Martha heard that Jesus was coming, and she went out to meet him.

MARTHA: [sadly] Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died. But even now, I know that God will give you whatever you ask.

JESUS: Your brother will rise again.

MARTHA: I know he will rise again, on the last day.

JESUS: [calm, strong] I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me will live, even though they die. Do you believe this?

MARTHA: Yes, Lord. I believe that you are the Christ, the Son of God.

NARRATOR: Then Mary came too. She fell down at Jesus's feet, and she was crying. She said the very same thing as Martha. The friends who had come with her were crying as well.

JESUS: Where have you laid him?

NARRATOR: They said, "Lord, come and see."

NARRATOR: [quietly] And Jesus wept.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: The tomb was a cave, with a big stone across the opening.

JESUS: Take away the stone.

MARTHA: [worried] But Lord, he has been dead for four days. There will be a bad smell.

JESUS: [gently] Did I not tell you? If you believe, you will see the glory of God.

NARRATOR: So they took away the stone. Jesus looked up, and he prayed to his Father. Then he called out, in a loud voice,

JESUS: [loud, commanding] Lazarus, come out!

[PAUSE 1.2]

NARRATOR: And the man who had died came out. He walked out of the tomb! His hands and his feet were still wrapped in strips of cloth. There was a cloth around his face.

JESUS: [warmly] Unwrap him, and let him go.

NARRATOR: Lazarus had been dead. Now he was alive. Jesus had called him by his name, and he came.

NARRATOR: Jesus is the resurrection and the life. Even death must do what Jesus says.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of John, chapter eleven.
