# Fidelity notes
# - The three deaths in chapter 1 are stated plainly, once.
# - Spoken lines are close paraphrase of 1:16-17, 1:20-21, 2:8-12, 2:20 and
#   the women's blessing in 4:14-15 (given to the narrator).
# - Naomi's bitterness against God (1:20-21) is kept, not smoothed over.
# - "Redeemer" is explained as a relative who could help the family (2:20).
# - Omitted for length: Orpah, the night at the threshing floor (ch. 3), the
#   nearer relative and the sandal at the town gate (4:1-12).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Ruth and Naomi.

NARRATOR: There was a woman named Naomi, who came from the town of Bethlehem. Once, there was no food in Bethlehem. So Naomi went to live in a far country, called Moab, with her husband and her two sons.

NARRATOR: But in Moab, Naomi's husband died. Her sons grew up and got married. And then her two sons died as well. Naomi had nobody left but her sons' wives. One of them was named Ruth.

NARRATOR: Then Naomi heard that God had given food to Bethlehem again. So she set off for home. She told Ruth to go back to her own mother's house. But Ruth held on to her tightly.

RUTH: [earnest] Do not tell me to leave you. Where you go, I will go. Where you stay, I will stay. Your people will be my people, and your God will be my God.

NARRATOR: So the two of them walked on together, all the way to Bethlehem. The women of the town came out to see.

NAOMI: [bitter, weary] Do not call me Naomi any more. I went away full, and the Lord has brought me back empty.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Naomi and Ruth were poor, and they needed food. It was harvest time. So Ruth went out to the fields, to pick up the grain the workers left behind.

NARRATOR: The field belonged to a man named Boaz. He saw Ruth, and he asked who she was. Then he spoke to her.

BOAZ: [kindly] Stay here, in my field. Nobody will hurt you. And when you are thirsty, drink the water my men have drawn.

RUTH: Why are you so kind to me? I am a stranger here.

BOAZ: I have heard about everything you have done for Naomi. May the Lord, the God of Israel, repay you. You have come to shelter under his wings.

NARRATOR: And Boaz told his workers to drop extra grain on purpose, for Ruth to find. That evening, Ruth carried home a great big load. Naomi asked whose field she had worked in. And Ruth told her about Boaz.

NAOMI: [glad] The Lord has not stopped being kind! That man is one of our own family.

NARRATOR: Boaz was a relative who could help them. And he did. Boaz married Ruth, and he took care of Naomi too.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Then God gave Ruth and Boaz a baby boy. Naomi held him in her lap. And the women of Bethlehem said, "Praise the Lord, Naomi! He has not left you alone."

NARRATOR: They named the baby Obed. And when Obed grew up, he had a son called Jesse. And Jesse had a son called David, who became the great king of Israel.

NARRATOR: Naomi thought she had come home empty. But God had not forgotten her. He filled her arms again.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Ruth.
