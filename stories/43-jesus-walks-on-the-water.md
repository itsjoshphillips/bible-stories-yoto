# Fidelity notes
# - Follows Matthew, the only Gospel that records Peter walking on the water.
# - The disciples' fear and their cry "It is a ghost!" (14:26) are kept.
# - Peter's sinking and Jesus's rebuke "You of little faith, why did you
#   doubt?" (14:31) are kept; the rescue comes first, as in the text.
# - Ends, as Matthew does, with worship: "Truly you are the Son of God."
# - "Very early in the morning, while it was still dark" renders "the fourth
#   watch of the night" (14:25), roughly three to six in the morning.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Jesus Walks on the Water.

NARRATOR: Jesus told his friends to get into the boat, and to go on ahead of him across the lake. Then he sent the crowds home. And Jesus went up on a mountain, all by himself, to pray.

NARRATOR: Evening came. Jesus was there alone. And the boat was far, far out from the land. The wind was blowing against it, and the waves were knocking it about.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Very early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus came to them. He came walking on the water.

NARRATOR: His friends saw someone walking on the lake, and they were terrified.

DISCIPLE: [crying out in fear] It is a ghost!

NARRATOR: But straight away, Jesus called to them.

JESUS: [warm, steady] Take heart. It is I. Do not be afraid.

NARRATOR: Then Peter called back.

PETER: Lord, if it is you, tell me to come to you on the water.

JESUS: Come.

NARRATOR: So Peter climbed out of the boat. And Peter walked on the water! Step, by step, by step, he came towards Jesus.

NARRATOR: But then Peter saw the wind. He was afraid. And he began to sink.

PETER: [shouting, frightened] Lord, save me!

NARRATOR: At once, Jesus reached out his hand, and caught him.

JESUS: [gently] You have so little faith. Why did you doubt?

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: They climbed into the boat together. And the wind stopped.

NARRATOR: Then everyone in the boat bowed down and worshipped Jesus. They said,

DISCIPLE: [awed] Truly, you are the Son of God.

NARRATOR: Jesus walked on the water. He caught Peter when he sank. And the wind stopped for him. Truly, he is the Son of God.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Matthew, chapter fourteen.
