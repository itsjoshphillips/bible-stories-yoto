# Fidelity notes
# - Spoken lines are close paraphrase: the loud voice from the throne
#   (21:3-4) and the words of the one seated on the throne (21:5-7).
# - John is introduced as a friend of Jesus who was shown these things by
#   Jesus (Revelation 1:1, 1:9). The script does not say how old he was.
# - Explanatory glosses, flagged for parental review: "the Lamb" is explained
#   as a name for Jesus (John 1:29; Revelation 5:6-9); the tree of life is
#   linked back to the garden (Genesis 2:9; 3:22-24); "They will see his
#   face" (22:4) is applied to "everyone who belongs to him".
# - The closing echo of story 1 ("In the beginning, God made everything")
#   is a framing device for the card, resting on 21:5-6.
# - Simplified: "I am the Alpha and the Omega" becomes "I am the beginning
#   and the end", the phrase that follows it in 21:6.
# - Omitted: "the sea was no more" (21:1), the city as a bride (21:2), the
#   warning of 21:8, God's name on their foreheads (22:4), the leaves for
#   the healing of the nations (22:2).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is the very last story in the Bible. It is called: God Makes Everything New.

NARRATOR: John was a friend of Jesus. One day, Jesus showed John something wonderful. He showed him what God is going to do, at the end of everything. And John wrote it all down.

NARRATOR: John saw a new heaven, and a new earth. The old ones had passed away. And he saw a shining city, God's own city, coming down out of heaven from God. Then John heard a loud voice from God's throne.

VOICE: [strong, joyful] Look! God's home is now with people. He will live with them. They will be his people, and God himself will be with them.

VOICE: [tenderly] He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death. No more sadness. No more crying. No more pain. All the old things have gone away.

NARRATOR: And then God, who sits on the throne, spoke.

GOD: [calm, warm] Look. I am making everything new.

[PAUSE 1.0]

GOD: Write this down, for these words are true, and you can trust them. It is done. I am the beginning, and the end. To anyone who is thirsty, I will give the water of life, as a free gift. I will be their God, and they will be my children.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Then John was shown a river. It was the river of the water of life, as clear and bright as glass. It flowed from the throne of God and of the Lamb. The Lamb is a name for Jesus.

NARRATOR: Beside the river grew the tree of life. Long ago, that tree grew in the very first garden. Now here it was again, heavy with fruit. It had twelve kinds of fruit, with fresh fruit every month.

NARRATOR: There will be nothing bad in God's city. Nothing at all. God's throne will be there, and his people will worship him. [with wonder] And they will see his face.

NARRATOR: There will be no more night. Nobody will need a lamp, or even the sun. The Lord God himself will be their light. And they will be with him for ever, and ever.

NARRATOR: In the beginning, God made everything, and it was very good. At the end, God will make everything new. And everyone who belongs to him will live with him, for ever.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Revelation, chapter twenty-one.
