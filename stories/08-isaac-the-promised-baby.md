# Fidelity notes
# - Genesis 18 says "the LORD appeared" and that Abraham saw "three men"; the
#   one who promises the son is called the LORD (18:13). The script keeps both
#   statements and gives the promise to the GOD voice.
# - The new names and the ages (ninety-nine and ninety) come from Genesis 17.
# - All speech is close paraphrase of 18:3-15 and 21:6-7. "Where is your wife,
#   Sarah?" is reported by the narrator because the text gives it to all three.
# - "Isaac means he laughs" is the meaning of the Hebrew name.
# - Omitted: the quantities of flour; the servant who cooks the calf; Isaac's
#   circumcision (21:4).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Isaac, the Promised Baby.

NARRATOR: [wistful] Years and years went by. God had promised Abram a son, but still no baby came. God gave Abram a new name, Abraham. He gave Sarai a new name, Sarah. But now Abraham was ninety-nine years old, and Sarah was ninety. They were far too old to have a baby.

NARRATOR: [lively] One hot day, Abraham was sitting at the door of his tent. He looked up, and there stood three men. The Lord had come to visit him. Abraham ran to meet them, and he bowed down low.

ABRAHAM: [eager] My lord, please do not pass by. Let me bring some water to wash your feet. Rest under this tree, and I will fetch you something to eat.

NARRATOR: [hurried, excited] Abraham hurried into the tent, to Sarah.

ABRAHAM: Quick! Take the best flour, and bake some bread!

NARRATOR: [busy, lively] Then he ran and chose a good, tender calf for the meat. He set the food in front of his visitors, with milk and cheese. And he stood near them, under the tree, while they ate.

NARRATOR: They asked him, "Where is your wife, Sarah?"

ABRAHAM: She is there, in the tent.

GOD: I will come back to you about this time next year. And Sarah, your wife, will have a son.

NARRATOR: [amused, hushed] Now, Sarah was listening at the door of the tent, just behind him. And when she heard that, she laughed to herself.

SARAH: [quietly, laughing to herself] A baby? Now that I am worn out, and my husband is old?

GOD: Why did Sarah laugh? Why did she say, "Can I really have a child, now that I am old?" Is anything too hard for the Lord? I will come back at the set time, next year, and Sarah will have a son.

NARRATOR: [quietly] Sarah was afraid.

SARAH: [nervous] I did not laugh.

GOD: [gently, firmly] Yes. You did laugh.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [joyful, amazed] And the Lord did just what he had promised. Sarah had a baby boy, at the very time God had said. Abraham was one hundred years old! He named his son Isaac. And Isaac means "he laughs".

SARAH: [joyful] God has brought me laughter! And everyone who hears about this will laugh with me. Who would ever have told Abraham that Sarah would feed a baby? Yet I have given him a son, in his old age!

NARRATOR: [warm, delighted] Is anything too hard for the Lord? No. Nothing at all. God had kept his promise.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Genesis, chapters eighteen and twenty-one.
