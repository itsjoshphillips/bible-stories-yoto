# Fidelity notes
# - Not softened: Cain kills his brother, and it is said in those words.
# - The text does not say why God accepted Abel's gift and not Cain's, so the
#   script does not say either. (Hebrews 11:4 says Abel offered "by faith".)
# - "The best parts of them" renders "their fat portions" (4:4).
# - Extends the planned passage by two verses (4:25-26, the birth of Seth), so
#   that the track ends on God's kindness rather than on Cain's exile. Eve's
#   closing speech is a close paraphrase of 4:25.
# - Omitted: Eve's words at Cain's birth (4:1); the sevenfold vengeance
#   (4:15); the name of the land of Nod; Cain's descendants (4:17-24).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is a sad story. It is called: Cain and Abel.

NARRATOR: Adam and Eve had two sons. The first was named Cain. The second was named Abel. When they grew up, Cain worked the ground, and grew crops. Abel looked after sheep.

NARRATOR: One day, each brother brought a gift to God. Cain brought some of the crops he had grown. Abel brought the firstborn lambs of his flock, and the best parts of them.

NARRATOR: God was pleased with Abel and his gift. But with Cain and his gift, God was not pleased. So Cain became very angry, and his face turned dark.

GOD: Why are you angry? Why is your face so dark? If you do what is right, will you not be accepted? But if you do not do what is right, sin is crouching at your door. It wants to have you. But you must rule over it.

NARRATOR: Cain did not rule over it. He spoke to his brother Abel, and they went out to the field. And there, in the field, Cain attacked his brother Abel, and killed him.

[PAUSE 1.5]

NARRATOR: Then the Lord said to Cain,

GOD: Where is Abel, your brother?

CAIN: [sullen] I do not know. Am I supposed to look after my brother?

GOD: [grieved, stern] What have you done? Your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground. Now, when you work the ground, it will no longer give you its crops. You will be a wanderer on the earth, with no home.

CAIN: [afraid] My punishment is more than I can bear! I will be hidden from your face. And whoever finds me will kill me!

GOD: No. That will not happen.

NARRATOR: And the Lord put a mark on Cain, so that no one who found him would kill him. Then Cain went away from the Lord, and he lived in a land to the east of Eden.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: After this, God gave Adam and Eve another son. Eve named him Seth.

EVE: God has given me another child in place of Abel, because Cain killed him.

NARRATOR: And in those days, people began to call on the name of the Lord.

NARRATOR: Sin had come into the world, and it had done a terrible thing. But God had not left his people. He heard them when they called.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Genesis, chapter four.
