# Fidelity notes
# - Follows Acts 9 in order; spoken lines are close paraphrase.
# - Stated plainly: Saul hated Jesus's followers and went to arrest them and
#   drag them to prison. Acts 9:1 also says he breathed "threats and murder";
#   the script says he wanted to stop them and put them in prison, and omits
#   the earlier death of Stephen (Acts 7:58-8:3).
# - Saul is voiced by the PAUL slot, so that he keeps one voice in story 59.
#   The change of name is explained in story 59, not here.
# - Simplified: Jesus's words to Ananias (9:15-16) are shortened to "I have
#   chosen him to carry my name to many people"; the suffering Saul will
#   face (9:16) is omitted.
# - Omitted: the street called Straight and the house of Judas (9:11), Saul's
#   vision of Ananias (9:12), the reaction of Saul's companions (9:7).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Saul Meets Jesus.

NARRATOR: There was a man named Saul, and Saul hated the followers of Jesus. He wanted to stop them all. So he set off for a city called Damascus. He planned to find the people there who followed Jesus, and tie them up, and take them away to prison.

NARRATOR: Saul was nearly at Damascus. Then, suddenly, a light from heaven flashed all around him. It was brighter than the sun. Saul fell to the ground. And he heard a voice.

JESUS: [calm, strong] Saul, Saul, why are you hurting me?

PAUL: [shaken] Who are you, Lord?

JESUS: I am Jesus, the one you are hurting. Now get up, and go into the city. There you will be told what you must do.

NARRATOR: Saul got up from the ground. He opened his eyes. But he could not see anything at all. The men who were with him had to take him by the hand, and lead him into Damascus. For three days Saul was blind, and he did not eat or drink.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Now, in Damascus there lived a follower of Jesus named Ananias. The Lord Jesus spoke to him.

JESUS: Ananias.

ANANIAS: Here I am, Lord.

JESUS: Get up, and go and find a man named Saul. He is praying.

ANANIAS: [worried] Lord, I have heard about this man. Many people have told me about the terrible things he has done to your people. And he has come here to arrest us!

JESUS: [gently, firmly] Go. I have chosen him to carry my name to many people.

NARRATOR: So Ananias went. He found the house, and he went in. He laid his hands on Saul.

ANANIAS: [kindly] Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus, who came to you on the road, has sent me. He sent me so that you can see again, and be filled with the Holy Spirit.

NARRATOR: At once, something like scales fell from Saul's eyes. He could see! He got up, and he was baptised. Then he had something to eat, and he grew strong again.

NARRATOR: And straight away, Saul began to tell everyone about Jesus.

PAUL: [bold, glad] Jesus is the Son of God!

NARRATOR: Everyone who heard him was amazed. Was this not the man who had come to arrest the followers of Jesus? It was. But Saul had met Jesus. And Jesus had made his enemy into his friend.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Acts, chapter nine.
