# Fidelity notes
# - Not softened: God's grief and judgement (6:5-7), and the death of every
#   person and animal outside the ark (7:21-23), are stated plainly, once.
# - God's speeches are close paraphrase of 6:13-21, 8:16-17, 8:22 and 9:11-16.
# - Noah never speaks in these chapters, so he never speaks here.
# - "Made an offering to God" stands for the burnt offerings of 8:20.
# - Omitted: the ark's measurements; the seven pairs of clean animals (7:2-3);
#   the raven (8:7); the dates and the 150 days; Noah's age; the rules about
#   food and bloodshed (9:1-7).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Noah and the Great Flood.

NARRATOR: [serious] Many, many years went by, and the world filled up with people. But the people had become wicked. All day long, they thought about doing evil. The earth was full of fighting and hurting.

NARRATOR: [softly, sad] God saw it. He was sorry that he had made people, and his heart was full of pain.

NARRATOR: [brightening] But there was one man who pleased God. His name was Noah. Noah did what was right, and he walked with God.

GOD: Noah, the earth is full of violence, and I am going to put an end to it. I will send a flood of water over the earth, and everything that lives on the earth will die. So build yourself an ark, a great big boat, made of wood. Make rooms inside it, and cover it with tar.

NARRATOR: Then God made a promise to Noah.

GOD: You will come into the ark: you, and your wife, and your sons, and their wives. Bring two of every kind of animal with you, a male and a female, to keep them alive. And take every kind of food.

NARRATOR: [cheerful] And Noah did everything God told him to do.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [lively, excited] When the ark was finished, Noah and his family went inside. And the animals came to Noah, two by two. Animals, and birds, and creeping things. Then the Lord shut the door.

[SFX: heavy rain beginning, distant rolling thunder | 4]

NARRATOR: [building, dramatic] And the rain came down. It rained for forty days and forty nights. Water burst up from under the ground, too. The water rose higher, and higher. It lifted the ark up off the ground. It covered even the highest mountains.

NARRATOR: [quietly, serious] Every person and every animal outside the ark died. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark.

[PAUSE 1.5]

NARRATOR: [gently, hopeful] But God remembered Noah, and all the animals in the ark. God sent a wind to blow across the earth, and the water began to go down. At last, the ark came to rest on a mountain.

NARRATOR: [lively] Noah opened a window, and sent out a dove. But the dove found nowhere to land, so it came back. Seven days later, Noah sent the dove out again. That evening it came back, and in its beak was a fresh green olive leaf! Seven days after that, Noah sent the dove a third time. And this time, it did not come back.

GOD: Come out of the ark, Noah. You, and your family, and every living thing.

NARRATOR: [glad] So out they came, onto the dry ground. Noah built an altar, and made an offering to God. Then God made a promise to Noah, and to every living thing.

GOD: [warmly] Never again will a flood destroy the earth. As long as the earth remains, there will be planting and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night. And here is the sign of my promise. I have put my rainbow in the clouds. When the rainbow appears, I will remember my promise.

NARRATOR: [warm, steady] God judged the wickedness of the world. And God kept Noah safe through the water. God always keeps his promises.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Genesis, chapters six to nine.
