# Fidelity notes
# - Follows Luke's order: teaching from the boat, the catch, Peter's
#   confession, the call. Spoken lines are close paraphrase of Luke.
# - Peter's "Go away from me, Lord. I am a sinful man" (5:8) is kept, not
#   softened into simple surprise.
# - "You will catch people" renders "you will be catching men" (5:10).
# - Luke calls him Simon and Simon Peter; the script says "Simon, who was also
#   called Peter" once and then uses Peter, to match the rest of the card.
# - Omitted: the name "lake of Gennesaret"; Zebedee as father of James and John.

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Jesus Calls the Fishermen.

NARRATOR: [setting the scene] One day, Jesus was standing beside a lake. A great crowd of people pushed in close around him. They wanted to hear the word of God.

NARRATOR: At the edge of the water, Jesus saw two boats. The fishermen had climbed out of them, and they were washing their nets. One of the boats belonged to a fisherman named Simon, who was also called Peter.

NARRATOR: Jesus stepped into Peter's boat, and asked him to push out a little way from the shore. Then Jesus sat down in the boat, and he taught the people from there.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: When Jesus had finished speaking, he turned to Peter.

JESUS: Go out into the deep water, and let down your nets for a catch.

PETER: [tired] Master, we worked hard all night long, and we caught nothing. Not one fish. But because you say so, I will let down the nets.

NARRATOR: [building excitement] So they let down the nets. And oh! The nets filled with fish. So many fish that the nets began to break!

NARRATOR: [amazed, delighted] They waved to their friends in the other boat to come and help. And they filled both boats with fish. They filled them so full that the boats began to sink.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [hushed] When Peter saw it, he fell down at Jesus's knees.

PETER: [shaken, humbly] Go away from me, Lord. I am a sinful man.

NARRATOR: [full of wonder] Peter was amazed at all the fish they had caught. So was everyone with him. So were his partners, James and John.

NARRATOR: [gently] But Jesus did not go away. He said to Peter,

JESUS: [gently] Do not be afraid. From now on, you will catch people.

NARRATOR: [lively, then quiet wonder] So they brought their boats back to the shore. And then they left everything. The boats. The nets. All those fish. They left it all behind, and they followed Jesus.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Luke, chapter five.
