# Fidelity notes
# - Speech is close paraphrase of Luke 2:10-12, 14-15.
# - Nothing is added from tradition: no donkey, no innkeeper, no stable and no
#   animals, because Luke mentions none of them. The manger (an animals' food
#   box) and "no room" are in the text (2:7).
# - "Christ" is kept and explained once as God's promised King.
# - "Terrified" stands for "filled with great fear" (2:9).
# - Omitted: Quirinius, governor of Syria (2:2).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Jesus Is Born.

NARRATOR: In those days, the ruler of all the lands was a man named Caesar Augustus. He gave an order. Everybody had to go to their own family's town, to be counted.

NARRATOR: So Joseph set off from Nazareth. He went all the way to Bethlehem, the town of King David, because he was from David's family. Mary went with him. And Mary was going to have her baby very soon.

NARRATOR: While they were in Bethlehem, the time came. Mary had her baby: her first son. She wrapped him up snugly in strips of cloth. And she laid him in a manger, a box where animals eat their food. There was no room for them in the place where travellers stayed.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: That night, out in the fields near Bethlehem, some shepherds were watching over their sheep. It was dark. It was quiet.

NARRATOR: Suddenly, an angel of the Lord stood in front of them! The bright glory of God shone all around them. The shepherds were terrified.

ANGEL: [warm, glad] Do not be afraid! I bring you good news of great joy, and it is for all the people. Today, in the town of David, a Saviour has been born for you. He is Christ the Lord: God's promised King. And this is how you will know him. You will find a baby, wrapped in cloths, and lying in a manger.

NARRATOR: Then, all at once, the sky was full of angels. More than anyone could count. And they were all praising God.

ANGEL: [joyful, ringing] Glory to God in the highest heaven! And peace on earth to the people he is pleased with!

[PAUSE 1.0]

NARRATOR: Then the angels went back to heaven. The fields were dark and quiet again. The shepherds looked at one another.

SHEPHERD: [eager] Come on! Let us go to Bethlehem, and see this thing that the Lord has told us about!

NARRATOR: They hurried off as fast as they could. And they found Mary, and Joseph. And there was the baby, lying in the manger. It was all just as the angel had said.

NARRATOR: The shepherds told everyone what the angel had said about this child. And everybody who heard it was amazed.

NARRATOR: But Mary kept all these things like treasure, and thought about them deep in her heart.

NARRATOR: Then the shepherds went back to their sheep. And all the way, they praised God for everything they had heard and seen.

NARRATOR: God had kept his promise. The Rescuer King had come. Not in a palace. In a manger.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Luke, chapter two.
