# Fidelity notes
# - Speech is close paraphrase of 1 Kings 17:1; 18:21, 24, 26-27, 36-39.
# - Elijah's teasing of the prophets of Baal (18:27) is kept; it is in the text.
# - Softened by parental decision: Elijah has the prophets of Baal put to death
#   at the brook Kishon (18:40). The script says only that every one of them
#   was defeated that day, which is true but does not state the killing.
# - Omitted as gore: the prophets of Baal cutting themselves (18:28).
# - Omitted for length: the widow of Zarephath and her son (17:8-24), Obadiah
#   (18:1-16), the seven trips to look for the cloud (18:43-44).
# - "Four hundred and fifty": 18:22.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Elijah and the Fire from Heaven.

NARRATOR: Israel had a king named Ahab. And King Ahab did a very bad thing. He stopped worshipping God. He bowed down to a pretend god, called Baal. And many of God's people did the same.

NARRATOR: So God sent a man named Elijah to the king.

ELIJAH: [firm] As surely as the Lord lives, there will be no rain in this land, and no dew, until I say so.

NARRATOR: And the rain stopped. The ground grew hard and dry. But God looked after Elijah. He hid him beside a little stream. And every morning and every evening, ravens came flying, with bread and meat for Elijah to eat.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: In the third year, Elijah went back to King Ahab. All the people came together on a mountain, called Mount Carmel. Four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal came too.

ELIJAH: How long will you keep hopping from one side to the other? If the Lord is God, follow him. If Baal is God, follow him.

NARRATOR: The people said nothing. Not a word.

ELIJAH: Then let us each build an altar, and put wood on it, but light no fire. You call on your god, and I will call on the Lord. The God who answers with fire, he is God.

CROWD: Yes. That is fair.

NARRATOR: The prophets of Baal went first. They called from morning until midday.

BAAL_PROPHET: [calling out] O Baal, answer us! O Baal, answer us!

NARRATOR: But there was no voice. Nobody answered. Elijah began to tease them.

ELIJAH: [dryly] Shout louder! Perhaps he is busy. Perhaps he is asleep, and you must wake him up!

NARRATOR: They shouted until evening. Still no voice. Nobody answered. Nobody was listening.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Then it was Elijah's turn. He built the Lord's altar with twelve stones. He dug a ditch all around it. He laid the wood on top. Then he had water poured over it all. Once. Twice. Three times! Water ran down the wood, and filled up the ditch.

NARRATOR: And Elijah prayed.

ELIJAH: [calm, clear] O Lord, God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel. Answer me, so that these people will know that you, Lord, are God, and that you are turning their hearts back to you.

[PAUSE 0.8]

NARRATOR: Then the fire of the Lord fell from heaven! It burned up the offering. It burned up the wood. It burned up the stones and the dust. It even licked up the water in the ditch.

NARRATOR: When the people saw it, they fell down with their faces to the ground.

CROWD: [awed] The Lord, he is God!

CROWD_2: [awed] The Lord, he is God!

NARRATOR: That day, every one of the prophets of Baal was defeated.

NARRATOR: Then a little cloud rose up out of the sea, as small as a man's hand. The sky grew black. The wind blew. And down came the rain.

NARRATOR: Baal could not answer, because Baal is not real. But the Lord is the living God. And he answered with fire.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the first book of Kings, chapter eighteen.
