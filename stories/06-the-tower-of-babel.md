# Fidelity notes
# - All speech is close paraphrase of 11:3-4 and 11:6-7. The people speak as a
#   group in the text; two voices share their lines.
# - God's "let us go down" keeps the plural of 11:7.
# - Interpretive gloss, flagged for parental review: "God had told people to
#   spread out and fill the earth" brings in Genesis 9:1 to explain why
#   refusing to be scattered was disobedience. Genesis 11 does not state it.
# - The opening sentence summarises Genesis 10.
# - Omitted: the name Shinar; "nothing they propose will be impossible" (11:6).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The Tower of Babel.

NARRATOR: [lively] After the flood, Noah's family grew, and grew, until there were many, many people again. And in those days, everyone in the whole world spoke the same language. Everybody used the same words.

NARRATOR: [setting the scene] The people travelled until they found a wide, flat plain. And there they settled down. They said to one another,

PEOPLE: Come on! Let us make bricks, and bake them hard.

NARRATOR: [busy, energetic] So they used bricks instead of stone, and sticky tar to hold the bricks together. Then they said,

PEOPLE_2: [excited] Come on! Let us build ourselves a city, with a tower that reaches right up to the sky!

PEOPLE: [proud] Let us make a great name for ourselves! Then we will not be scattered all over the earth.

NARRATOR: [knowing] Now, God had told people to spread out and fill the earth. But these people wanted to stay in one place, and make themselves great. So brick upon brick, the tower went up.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [hushed] But the Lord came down to see the city, and the tower the people were building.

GOD: Look. They are one people, and they all have one language. And this is only the beginning of what they will do. Come, let us go down and mix up their language, so that they will not understand one another.

NARRATOR: [amused] And that is what the Lord did. He mixed up their language. One builder spoke, and the next could not understand a word.

NARRATOR: [plainly] So the building stopped. And the Lord scattered the people from that place, all over the earth.

NARRATOR: The city was called Babel, because there the Lord mixed up the language of the whole world.

NARRATOR: [warm, sure] The people built a tower to make their own name great. But it is the Lord who is great, over all the earth.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Genesis, chapter eleven.
