# Fidelity notes
# - Follows the seven days in order. God's speech is close paraphrase of the text.
# - "And God saw that it was good" is deliberately absent from day two, as in Genesis.
# - "Let us make people in our image" keeps the plural of Genesis 1:26.
# - Compressed: the naming details of day four (signs, seasons, years); the
#   food given to the animals (1:30).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: God Makes Everything.

NARRATOR: [hushed, mysterious] In the beginning, there was no sun. No sky. No sea. No trees, no birds, no people. There was only God.

NARRATOR: [quiet, expectant] Everything was dark, and deep, and empty. And then, God spoke.

GOD: [calm, warm] Let there be light.

NARRATOR: [bright, delighted] And there was light! Bright, shining light. God called the light "day", and the dark he called "night". There was evening, and there was morning. That was the very first day. And God saw that the light was good.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: On the second day, God spoke again.

GOD: Let there be a great wide space, to part the waters above from the waters below.

NARRATOR: [calmly] And it was so. God called the wide space "sky".

NARRATOR: On the third day, God said,

GOD: Let the waters gather together, and let dry land appear.

NARRATOR: [lively] The waters rushed together, and up came the dry land. God called the land "earth", and the waters he called "seas". Then God said,

GOD: Let the earth grow green.

NARRATOR: [with wonder] And up came the grass, and plants with seeds, and trees heavy with fruit. And God saw that it was good.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: On the fourth day, God said,

GOD: Let there be lights in the sky.

NARRATOR: [with wonder] God made the big, bright sun to rule the day. He made the soft, silver moon to rule the night. And he made the stars too. Every single one. And God saw that it was good.

NARRATOR: On the fifth day, God said,

GOD: Let the seas be full of living things, and let birds fly across the sky.

NARRATOR: [playful, excited] Splash! Fish, and great sea creatures. Swoosh! Birds of every kind. And God saw that it was good. God blessed them, and said,

GOD: [gladly] Have many, many babies. Fill the seas, and fill the sky.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: On the sixth day, God said,

GOD: Let the earth bring out animals of every kind.

NARRATOR: [playful] Cows and sheep. Lions and lizards. Creeping things and leaping things. And God saw that it was good.

NARRATOR: [building anticipation] But God was not finished. He saved the most special thing for last.

GOD: [warmly] Let us make people in our image, to be like us.

NARRATOR: [warm, tender] So God made people. He made them in his own image: a man, and a woman. God blessed them, and he gave them a job to do.

GOD: Have children, and fill the earth. Rule over the fish, and the birds, and every animal. Look. I have given you every plant, and every fruit tree, for your food.

NARRATOR: [glad] Then God looked at everything he had made. And it was not just good. [with quiet delight] It was very good.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [calm, restful] On the seventh day, God's work was finished. So God rested. He blessed the seventh day, and made it holy: a special day, set apart for him.

NARRATOR: [warm, delighted] God made everything. He only had to speak, and it was so. And everything God made was good.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Genesis, chapter one.
