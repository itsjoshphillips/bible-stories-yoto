# Fidelity notes
# - Not softened: the Egyptian army drowns, "not one of them was left"
#   (14:28), and Israel sees it (14:30). Stated plainly, once each.
# - Spoken lines are close paraphrase of 14:5, 14:11, 14:13-14, 14:16, 14:25.
# - The pillar of cloud moving between the two camps (14:19-20) and the
#   strong east wind blowing all night (14:21) are kept.
# - Omitted for length: the route and place names, Joseph's bones (13:19),
#   the six hundred chosen chariots, the chariot wheels (14:25a), the song of
#   Moses and Miriam (ch. 15, outside the passage).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Through the Red Sea.

NARRATOR: God's people were free. They walked out of Egypt, and God himself showed them the way. By day, he went in front of them in a tall pillar of cloud. By night, he went in front of them in a pillar of fire, to give them light. And he led them to the edge of the sea.

NARRATOR: But back in Egypt, Pharaoh changed his mind.

PHARAOH: [angry] What have we done? We have let our slaves go!

NARRATOR: So Pharaoh called for his army. Horses, and chariots, and soldiers. And they raced after God's people.

[SFX: many horses galloping far off, growing nearer | 4]

NARRATOR: God's people looked up. And there was Pharaoh's army, coming closer and closer. In front of them was the deep sea. Behind them was the army. They were terribly afraid, and they cried out to God. Then they turned on Moses.

CROWD: [frightened, angry] Did you bring us out here to die?

MOSES: [steady] Do not be afraid. Stand still, and see how the Lord will save you today. The Lord will fight for you. You only need to be still.

NARRATOR: Then God spoke to Moses.

GOD: Lift up your staff, and stretch out your hand over the sea.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: The pillar of cloud moved. It went round behind God's people, and it stood between them and the army. All night long, the army could not come near.

NARRATOR: Moses stretched out his hand over the sea. And all night long, God sent a strong wind. It blew, and it blew. The sea split in two! The water stood up like a wall on the right, and like a wall on the left. And in the middle, there was a path of dry ground.

NARRATOR: So God's people walked through the sea. Every one of them, on dry ground.

NARRATOR: Pharaoh's army came charging in after them, right into the middle of the sea. But God threw the army into a muddle. The soldiers shouted,

CROWD_2: [panicked] Run! The Lord is fighting for Israel!

NARRATOR: Then God said to Moses,

GOD: Stretch out your hand over the sea again.

NARRATOR: Moses stretched out his hand. And as the morning came, the water rushed back. It covered the chariots, and the horses, and all of Pharaoh's army. Not one of them was left.

[PAUSE 1.0]

NARRATOR: God's people stood safe on the other side. They saw what the Lord had done. And they believed in the Lord, and in his servant Moses.

NARRATOR: The sea was too deep. The army was too strong. But the Lord fought for his people, and he saved them.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Exodus, chapter fourteen.
