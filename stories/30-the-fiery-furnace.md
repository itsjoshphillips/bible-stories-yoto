# Fidelity notes
# - Speech is close paraphrase of Daniel 3:4-6, 14-18, 24-26, 28-29.
# - Not softened: the threat of burning, "but even if he does not" (3:18), and
#   the death of the soldiers who threw the three men in (3:22), stated once.
# - "Like a son of the gods" (3:25) is kept as the king says it, and the king's
#   own explanation, "his angel" (3:28), is kept too. No further gloss is added
#   on who the fourth figure was.
# - The three friends answer together in the text; one voice speaks for them.
# - "Taller than a house": the statue is sixty cubits, about 27 metres (3:1).
# - Omitted for length: the list of officials and instruments, the accusers
#   (3:8-12), the king's decree of punishment (3:29b), the promotion (3:30).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The Fiery Furnace.

NARRATOR: God's people were living far from home, in the land of Babylon. The king of Babylon was named Nebuchadnezzar. He made a statue out of gold. It was taller than a house, and it shone in the sun. Then the king's messenger called out to all the people,

HERALD: [proclaiming] When you hear the music play, you must fall down and worship the golden statue. Whoever does not fall down will be thrown into a burning, fiery furnace!

NARRATOR: The music played. And all the people fell down and worshipped the statue. All but three. Their names were Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego. They worshipped God, and only God. So they stayed standing.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: The king was furious. He sent for the three friends.

NEBUCHADNEZZAR: [angry] Is it true? Do you not worship my golden statue? Bow down now, or you will be thrown into the fire. And then what god can save you from my hand?

SHADRACH: [steady, brave] O king, the God we serve is able to save us from the fire. He will save us from your hand. But even if he does not, we will never serve your gods. We will not worship your statue.

NARRATOR: The king's face twisted with rage. He ordered the furnace to be made seven times hotter. His strongest soldiers tied up the three friends, in all their clothes, and threw them in. The fire was so hot that it killed the soldiers who threw them.

[PAUSE 1.0]

NARRATOR: Then the king jumped to his feet. He stared into the fire.

NEBUCHADNEZZAR: [astonished] Did we not throw three men into the fire, tied up tight? Look! I see four men. They are walking about in the fire, untied, and they are not hurt. And the fourth one looks like a son of the gods!

NARRATOR: The king went close to the door of the furnace.

NEBUCHADNEZZAR: [calling] Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, servants of the Most High God! Come out!

NARRATOR: And out they walked. Everybody crowded round to look. The fire had not hurt them at all. Not one hair on their heads was burnt. Their clothes were not burnt. They did not even smell of smoke.

NEBUCHADNEZZAR: [awed] Praise be to the God of Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego! He sent his angel, and he saved his servants who trusted him. No other god can save like this.

NARRATOR: The three friends would not bow down to anyone but God. And in the middle of the fire, God was with them.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Daniel, chapter three.
