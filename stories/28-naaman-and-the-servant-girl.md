# Fidelity notes
# - Speech is close paraphrase of 2 Kings 5:3, 10-13, 15-16, 19.
# - Not softened: the girl was carried away from her home by soldiers (5:2).
# - "Leprosy" is kept and explained once as a skin sickness.
# - "Like the skin of a little child": 5:14.
# - Omitted for length: the letter to the king of Israel and his torn clothes
#   (5:5-8), Naaman's request for earth and his question about the temple of
#   Rimmon (5:17-18), and Gehazi's greed (5:20-27, outside the reference).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Naaman and the Servant Girl.

NARRATOR: In the land of Syria, there lived a great soldier named Naaman. He was the leader of the king's whole army. He was strong and brave. But Naaman was sick. He had leprosy: a sickness that covered his skin with sores. And nobody could make him well.

NARRATOR: Now, in Naaman's house there lived a little girl from Israel. Soldiers from Syria had carried her away from her home. Now she was a servant to Naaman's wife. One day, the little girl said,

GIRL: [earnestly] I wish my master would go to the prophet in Israel! He would make him well.

NARRATOR: So Naaman went. He took his horses and his chariots, and he rode all the way to Israel. He stopped at the door of the prophet's house. The prophet's name was Elisha.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: But Elisha did not come out. He sent a messenger instead.

MESSENGER: Go and wash in the River Jordan seven times. Then your skin will be made new, and you will be clean.

NARRATOR: Naaman was angry.

NAAMAN: [offended] I thought he would come out to me! I thought he would call on the name of the Lord his God, and wave his hand, and make me well. And we have better rivers at home than any river in Israel!

NARRATOR: He turned around, and went away in a rage. But his servants came close, and spoke to him gently.

SERVANT: [gently] Master, if the prophet had told you to do something great and hard, would you not have done it? All he said was, "Wash, and be clean."

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: So Naaman went down to the River Jordan. He dipped himself under the water. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. [slowly] Seven times.

NARRATOR: And when he came up, his skin was made new. It was as clean and soft as the skin of a little child. Naaman was well!

NARRATOR: He went straight back to Elisha, with all his men. And this time, he stood before him.

NAAMAN: [humbled, glad] Now I know that there is no God in all the world, except the God of Israel. Please, take a present from me.

ELISHA: As surely as the Lord lives, I will not take anything.

NARRATOR: Naaman asked again. But Elisha would not take it.

ELISHA: [warmly] Go in peace.

NARRATOR: A little servant girl, far from home, knew where help could be found. And a great soldier found out that there is only one true God.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the second book of Kings, chapter five.
