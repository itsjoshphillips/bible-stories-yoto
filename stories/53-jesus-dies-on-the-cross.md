# Fidelity notes
# - Not softened: the beating, the thorns, the nails, the mockery and the death
#   are all stated plainly, once each, without lingering description.
# - Harmonised from three Gospels. Sources for each saying: "Father, forgive
#   them" and the robber (Luke 23:34, 42-43); "It is finished" (John 19:30);
#   "into your hands" (Luke 23:46); the centurion (Mark 15:39).
# - Doctrinal gloss, flagged for parental review: the paragraph beginning "Why
#   did Jesus die?" rests on John 10:18, 1 Corinthians 15:3 and 1 Peter 3:18.
# - The closing promise is Jesus's own prediction (Mark 8:31, Luke 18:33), so
#   the track never ends at a sealed tomb with no hope.
# - Omitted: Herod (Luke 23:6-12), Barabbas, Simon of Cyrene, the dividing of
#   clothes, the sign over the cross, "My God, my God" (Mark 15:34).

NARRATOR: [warm, gentle storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is a sad story, and a very important one. It is called: Jesus Dies on the Cross.

NARRATOR: [gently, serious] Jesus had never done anything wrong. Not one single thing. But the leaders of the people hated him. They tied him up, and they took him to the ruler, a man named Pilate.

PILATE: I have looked into this. And this man has done nothing wrong.

NARRATOR: [quietly, tense] But a great crowd had gathered. And the crowd began to shout.

CROWD: [shouting] Crucify him!

NARRATOR: [softly, sad] So Pilate gave them what they wanted. The soldiers took Jesus away. They beat him, and they laughed at him. They pressed a crown of sharp thorns onto his head.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [quietly] Then they led Jesus out to a place called the Skull. There, the soldiers nailed Jesus to a big wooden cross, and they lifted it up high. Two robbers were put on crosses too, one on each side of him.

NARRATOR: [tenderly] It hurt terribly. But listen to what Jesus said.

JESUS: [in pain, tenderly] Father, forgive them. They do not know what they are doing.

NARRATOR: [softly, sad] People stood and watched. And some of them made fun of him.

CROWD: [mocking] He saved other people. Let him save himself!

NARRATOR: [quietly] But Jesus stayed on the cross.

NARRATOR: [gently] One of the robbers turned his head towards Jesus.

ROBBER: [weak, pleading] Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom.

JESUS: [gently] I tell you the truth. Today you will be with me in paradise.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [hushed] Then, in the middle of the day, when the sun should have been at its brightest, the whole land went dark. For three long hours, it was dark.

NARRATOR: [quietly] And then Jesus called out, in a loud voice,

JESUS: [loud, strong] It is finished! [quietly] Father, into your hands I give my spirit.

NARRATOR: [slowly, quietly] And Jesus died.

NARRATOR: [hushed, with wonder] At that very moment, in God's great temple, the thick, heavy curtain was torn in two, from the top all the way down to the bottom. And a soldier, standing right beside the cross, who had seen it all, said,

SOLDIER: [awed] Truly, this man was the Son of God.

NARRATOR: [gently] That evening, a good man named Joseph came. He took Jesus's body down from the cross. He wrapped it in clean linen cloth, and he laid it in a tomb: a cave, cut into the rock. Then a great, heavy stone was rolled across the door. The women who had followed Jesus watched, and they saw where he was laid.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [tenderly] Why did Jesus die? He did not have to. He chose to. Jesus died to take away our sins: all the wrong things that keep us far from God. He did it because he loves us.

NARRATOR: [gently, with hope] Jesus's friends thought it was the end. But it was not the end. Jesus had made a promise. On the third day, he would rise again.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Luke, chapter twenty-three.
