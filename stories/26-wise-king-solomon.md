# Fidelity notes
# - Spoken lines are close paraphrase of 3:5-13, 8:23, 8:27-30 and 8:56.
# - Judgement call, flagged for parental review: the two mothers and the
#   order to cut the living baby in two (3:16-28) is omitted. It is the
#   passage's own proof of Solomon's wisdom, but the threat is a frightening
#   picture for under-fives and the plan does not mark this story as
#   weighty. It could be added as a second scene.
# - "I am only a little child" is Solomon's own phrase (3:7).
# - The cloud filling the temple so that the priests could not stand to do
#   their work is from 8:10-11.
# - Omitted for length: the measurements and furnishings of the temple, the
#   golden cherubim, the full length of Solomon's prayer, the sacrifices.

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Wise King Solomon.

NARRATOR: [setting the scene] David was king for many years. When he died, his son Solomon became king after him.

NARRATOR: [hushed] One night, God came to Solomon in a dream.

GOD: [calm, warm] Ask. What shall I give you?

NARRATOR: [curious] Solomon could have asked for anything in the whole world. And this is what he said.

SOLOMON: [humbly] Lord, you have made me king, like my father David. But I am only a little child. I do not know how to do it. So please give me a wise heart, to lead your people, and to know right from wrong.

NARRATOR: [warm] God was pleased with what Solomon asked.

GOD: You did not ask for a long life. You did not ask to be rich. You asked to be wise. So I will give you a wise heart. There has never been anyone as wise as you, and there never will be. And I will give you what you did not ask for too: riches, and honour.

NARRATOR: [brightening] Then Solomon woke up. And it was a dream. But God did just as he had said.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [grand, with relish] Then King Solomon began a great work. He built a house for God, in the city of Jerusalem. It was called the temple.

NARRATOR: [hushed, with wonder] It was made of great stones, cut and shaped far away. So no hammer was heard in the temple while it was being built. Inside, the walls were covered with sweet-smelling cedar wood. And over the wood, there was gold. Gold on the walls. Gold on the floor. It took seven years to build.

NARRATOR: [reverent] When it was finished, the priests carried in the special golden box that held God's commandments. They set it down in the most holy place.

NARRATOR: [slow, awed] And then a cloud filled the temple. The priests could not even stand up to do their work. For the glory of the Lord filled the house of the Lord.

[PAUSE 1.0]

NARRATOR: [quietly] King Solomon stood in front of all the people. He lifted his hands up towards heaven, and he prayed.

SOLOMON: [awed] O Lord, God of Israel, there is no God like you, in heaven above or on the earth below. You keep your promises, and you love your people. But will God really live on the earth? Even the highest heaven is not big enough to hold you. How much less this house that I have built!

SOLOMON: Yet hear us, Lord, when we pray towards this place. Hear us in heaven, where you live. And when you hear, forgive.

NARRATOR: [warm] Then Solomon turned, and he blessed the people.

SOLOMON: [gladly] Praise the Lord! He has given his people rest, just as he said. Not one word of all his good promises has failed.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the first book of Kings, chapter eight.
