# Fidelity notes
# - Follows Acts 2 in order. The signs are described with Luke's own
#   comparisons: a sound "like" a rushing wind, tongues "as of" fire.
# - Peter's sermon (2:14-36) is heavily compressed to its core claims, each a
#   close paraphrase: this is what Joel promised (2:16-17); you crucified
#   Jesus, God raised him, we are witnesses (2:23-24, 32); God has made him
#   Lord and Christ (2:36). "Christ" is explained as "God's promised King".
# - Peter's charge "you put him to death" is kept, as he says it to the
#   Jerusalem crowd.
# - Omitted: the list of nations (2:9-11), the mockers who say the disciples
#   are drunk and Peter's reply (2:13-15), the quotations from the Psalms.
# - "Repent" is rendered "turn back to God".

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The Holy Spirit Comes.

NARRATOR: Jesus had gone up to heaven. Before he went, he had told his friends to wait in Jerusalem for a gift: the Holy Spirit. So they waited.

NARRATOR: Then came a special day, called the day of Pentecost. All of Jesus's friends were together in one house.

[SFX: sudden strong rushing wind filling a room | 4]

NARRATOR: Suddenly, a sound came from heaven. It was like a mighty, rushing wind, and it filled the whole house. Then they saw something that looked like little flames of fire. A flame came to rest on each one of them.

NARRATOR: They were all filled with the Holy Spirit. And they began to speak in other languages! The Holy Spirit gave them the words to say.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Now, Jerusalem was full of visitors, from countries all over the world. When they heard the sound, a great crowd came running. And they could hardly believe their ears.

CROWD: [amazed] Aren't all these people from Galilee? Then how is it that each of us hears them in our own language?

CROWD_2: [amazed] They are telling the great things God has done! What does this mean?

NARRATOR: Then Peter stood up, with the other eleven disciples, and he spoke to the crowd in a loud voice.

PETER: [bold, clear] Listen to me! This is what God promised long ago: "I will pour out my Spirit on all people." You know about Jesus. You put him to death on a cross. But God raised him up to life again, and all of us have seen him. So know this for certain. God has made Jesus both Lord and Christ: God's promised King.

NARRATOR: When the people heard this, it cut them to the heart.

CROWD: [troubled] Brothers, what should we do?

PETER: [warmly] Turn back to God, and be baptised, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ. Your sins will be forgiven, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. This promise is for you, and for your children, and for all who are far away.

NARRATOR: That day, about three thousand people believed, and were baptised. Three thousand new friends of Jesus, in one day!

NARRATOR: Jesus had kept his promise. He had sent the Holy Spirit, to live in his people, and to make them brave.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Acts, chapter two.
