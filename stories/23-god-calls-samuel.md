# Fidelity notes
# - Spoken lines are close paraphrase of 1:11, 1:17, 1:27-28, 3:4-10, 3:18.
# - Not softened: God's message to Samuel is one of judgement on Eli's
#   family (3:11-14). It is stated plainly in one sentence, and Eli's
#   reply (3:18) is kept.
# - The boy is voiced as YOUNG_SAMUEL so that the grown prophet in story 24
#   can have an older voice. The name spoken aloud is simply Samuel.
# - "Samuel sounds like Asked of God" follows Hannah's own explanation (1:20).
# - Omitted for length: Elkanah and Peninnah (1:1-8), Eli mistaking Hannah
#   for drunk (1:12-16), Hannah's song (ch. 2), the detail of what Eli's
#   sons did (2:12-17).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: God Calls Samuel.

NARRATOR: There was a woman named Hannah. More than anything, Hannah wanted a baby. But year after year, no baby came. And Hannah was very sad.

NARRATOR: One day, Hannah went to God's house, in a place called Shiloh. She cried, and she prayed.

HANNAH: [weeping, quietly] O Lord, please look at how sad I am. Remember me, and give me a son. Then I will give him back to you, for his whole life.

NARRATOR: An old priest named Eli was sitting by the door.

ELI: Go in peace. And may the God of Israel give you what you have asked.

NARRATOR: And God remembered Hannah. She had a baby boy! She named him Samuel, which sounds like "Asked of God". When Samuel was old enough, Hannah kept her promise. She brought him to Eli, at God's house.

HANNAH: [glad] I prayed for this child, and the Lord gave me what I asked. So now I give him to the Lord, for as long as he lives.

NARRATOR: So Samuel lived at God's house, and he helped old Eli.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: One night, Eli was lying down in his place. His eyes were weak, and he could hardly see. Samuel was lying down too, in God's house. The lamp was still burning. [softly] And the Lord called.

GOD: Samuel!

NARRATOR: Samuel jumped up, and ran to Eli.

YOUNG_SAMUEL: Here I am! You called me.

ELI: [sleepy] I did not call you. Go back and lie down.

NARRATOR: So Samuel lay down. And the Lord called again.

GOD: Samuel!

YOUNG_SAMUEL: Here I am! You called me.

ELI: I did not call you, my son. Lie down again.

NARRATOR: Samuel did not yet know the Lord's voice. Then the Lord called a third time. And Samuel went to Eli a third time. Now Eli understood who was calling the boy.

ELI: [gently] Go and lie down. And if he calls you, say this: "Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening."

NARRATOR: Samuel lay down in his place. And the Lord came, and stood there, and called as before.

GOD: Samuel! Samuel!

YOUNG_SAMUEL: [hushed] Speak, for your servant is listening.

NARRATOR: Then God told Samuel a hard thing. Eli's sons had done great wrong, and Eli had not stopped them. So God was going to punish Eli's family.

NARRATOR: In the morning, Samuel was afraid to tell Eli. But Eli asked him, and Samuel told him everything.

ELI: [quietly] He is the Lord. Let him do what he knows is good.

NARRATOR: Samuel grew up, and the Lord was with him. Samuel listened to God, and he told the people what God said. And all Israel knew that God spoke through Samuel.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the first book of Samuel, chapter three.
