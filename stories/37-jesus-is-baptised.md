# Fidelity notes
# - Speech is close paraphrase of Matthew 3:2, 11, 14-15, 17; 4:3-4, 9-10.
# - "Turn back to God" stands for "repent"; "everything that God says is
#   right" for "to fulfil all righteousness" (3:15).
# - Not softened: the devil is named, and he tempts Jesus in person.
# - Compressed: three temptations to two. The temple temptation (4:5-7) is
#   omitted for length; the count is not stated, so nothing false is said.
# - Jesus's answers are his own quotations of Deuteronomy 8:3 and 6:13.
# - Omitted: John's warning to the Pharisees and Sadducees (3:7-10, 12).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Jesus Is Baptised.

NARRATOR: Jesus grew up, and became a man. And in those days, there was a preacher named John, who lived out in the wild, dry desert. John wore clothes made of camel's hair, with a leather belt. He ate locusts and wild honey. And this is what he said.

JOHN_BAPTIST: [calling out] Turn back to God! The kingdom of heaven is near!

NARRATOR: People came to John from all around. They said sorry to God for the wrong things they had done. And John baptised them in the River Jordan. He dipped them down under the water, and brought them up again.

JOHN_BAPTIST: I baptise you with water. But someone is coming after me who is far greater than I am. I am not good enough even to carry his sandals.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Then, one day, Jesus came to the river, to be baptised by John. John tried to stop him.

JOHN_BAPTIST: [astonished] I need to be baptised by you! Why do you come to me?

JESUS: [gently] Let it be this way for now. It is right for us to do everything that God says is right.

NARRATOR: So John baptised Jesus. And as Jesus came up out of the water, the sky opened! He saw the Spirit of God coming down like a dove, and resting on him. And a voice spoke from heaven.

GOD: [warm, glad] This is my Son, whom I love. I am very pleased with him.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Then God's Spirit led Jesus out into the desert. For forty days and forty nights, Jesus ate nothing at all. He was very hungry. And there, the devil came to him, to try to make him do wrong.

DEVIL: [sly] If you are the Son of God, tell these stones to turn into bread.

JESUS: [steady] It is written in God's word: people do not live on bread alone. They live on every word that God speaks.

NARRATOR: The devil tried again. He took Jesus up a very high mountain, and showed him all the kingdoms of the world, and all their riches.

DEVIL: [smooth] I will give you all of this, if you will bow down and worship me.

JESUS: [firm] Go away, Satan! It is written: worship the Lord your God, and serve only him.

NARRATOR: Then the devil left him. And angels came, and took care of Jesus.

NARRATOR: Every time the devil tried, Jesus said no. Jesus never did anything wrong. He is God's own Son, whom God loves.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Matthew, chapters three and four.
