# Fidelity notes
# - Follows Matthew closely. Jesus's instructions and the crowd's shouts are
#   close paraphrase of 21:2-3 and 21:9-11.
# - The promise quoted is Zechariah 9:9, which Matthew himself cites (21:4-5).
# - Matthew says "branches from the trees"; palm branches are named only in
#   John 12:13, so the script says "branches".
# - Explanatory gloss, flagged for parental review: "Hosanna" explained as
#   "Save us!" (the Hebrew behind the word, see Psalm 118:25-26), and "Son of
#   David" explained as the King God promised (2 Samuel 7:12-16).
# - The closing line that the King came to die points on to story 53; it is
#   implied by the Gospel's sequence, not stated in this passage.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The King Rides In.

NARRATOR: Jesus and his disciples were walking to the great city of Jerusalem. When they came near, to the hill called the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent two of his disciples ahead.

JESUS: Go into the village in front of you. Straight away you will find a donkey tied up, and her young one with her. Untie them, and bring them to me. If anyone says anything to you, tell them, "The Lord needs them." And he will send them at once.

NARRATOR: The two disciples went. And it was just as Jesus had said. They brought the donkey and her young one to Jesus. They laid their cloaks on them, and Jesus sat on top.

NARRATOR: Now, long, long before, God had made a promise through one of his messengers. This is what the promise said.

GOD: [warm, steady] Look, your king is coming to you. He is gentle, and he is riding on a donkey.

NARRATOR: And here he came! Not on a tall war horse. On a donkey. Clip, clop. Clip, clop. Down the road to Jerusalem.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: A very great crowd came out to see. People took off their cloaks, and spread them on the road in front of Jesus. Others cut branches from the trees, and spread those on the road too.

NARRATOR: There were crowds in front of him. There were crowds behind him. And they all began to shout.

CROWD: [shouting, joyful] Hosanna to the Son of David!

CROWD_2: [shouting, joyful] Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord!

CROWD: [shouting] Hosanna in the highest!

NARRATOR: The word "Hosanna" means: save us! And "Son of David" was a name for the great King whom God had promised to send.

NARRATOR: So Jesus rode into Jerusalem. The whole city was stirred up. Everyone was asking,

CROWD_2: [curious] Who is this?

NARRATOR: And the crowds answered,

CROWD: This is Jesus, the prophet, from Nazareth in Galilee.

NARRATOR: The King had come, just as God had promised. A gentle King, riding on a donkey. And he had come to Jerusalem to save his people.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Matthew, chapter twenty-one.
