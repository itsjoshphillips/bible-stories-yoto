# Fidelity notes
# - God's and Moses's lines are close paraphrase of Exodus 3:4-14 and 4:1-17.
# - The text says "the angel of the LORD appeared to him in a flame of fire"
#   (3:2) and then "God called to him out of the bush" (3:4). The script
#   uses God's voice throughout.
# - "I AM WHO I AM" (3:14) is kept and left unexplained, as in the text.
# - God's anger at Moses's last refusal (4:14) is kept in one plain sentence.
# - Background sentence from outside the passage: the people's cry reaching
#   God (2:23-25).
# - Omitted for length: why Moses left Egypt (2:11-22), Jethro's name, the
#   hand turned leprous (4:6-7), water turned to blood (4:9), the promise of
#   plunder (3:21-22).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Moses and the Burning Bush.

NARRATOR: Moses was a grown man now. He lived far away from Egypt, and he was a shepherd. But back in Egypt, God's people were still slaves. They cried out for help. And God heard them.

NARRATOR: One day, Moses led his sheep to a mountain. And there he saw a strange thing. A bush was on fire. It burned, and it burned. But it did not burn up.

MOSES: [curious] I must go and look at this. Why does the bush not burn up?

NARRATOR: Then God called to him, out of the bush.

GOD: Moses! Moses!

MOSES: Here I am.

GOD: [calm, warm] Do not come any closer. Take off your sandals, for the place where you are standing is holy ground. I am the God of your father. The God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.

NARRATOR: Moses hid his face. He was afraid to look at God.

[SCENE]

GOD: I have seen how my people suffer in Egypt. I have heard them crying. And I have come down to rescue them, and to bring them to a good, wide land. So come. I am sending you to Pharaoh, to bring my people out of Egypt.

MOSES: [dismayed] Who am I, to go to Pharaoh?

GOD: I will be with you.

MOSES: But if the people ask me, "What is his name?", what shall I tell them?

GOD: [slowly] I AM WHO I AM. Tell them this: I AM has sent me to you.

NARRATOR: Still, Moses was not sure.

MOSES: What if they do not believe me?

GOD: What is that in your hand?

MOSES: A staff.

GOD: Throw it on the ground.

NARRATOR: Moses threw down his shepherd's staff. And it turned into a snake! Moses ran away from it.

GOD: Reach out your hand, and catch it by the tail.

NARRATOR: Moses reached out, and he caught it. And it was a staff again, in his hand. That was a sign, to show the people that God had truly sent him.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: But Moses still did not want to go.

MOSES: [pleading] Oh, my Lord, I am not good at speaking. My words come slowly.

GOD: Who made your mouth? Was it not I, the Lord? Now go. I will help you speak, and I will teach you what to say.

MOSES: Oh, my Lord, please send somebody else.

NARRATOR: Then God was angry with Moses. But he did not change his plan.

GOD: [firm] Your brother Aaron speaks well. He is already on his way to meet you. He will speak for you, and I will help you both. And take your staff in your hand.

NARRATOR: Moses did not feel brave, and he did not feel ready. But God had seen his people. God had heard them. And God was coming to rescue them.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Exodus, chapter three.
