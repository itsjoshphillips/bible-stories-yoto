# Fidelity notes
# - Tells the whole parable, including the older brother (15:25-32), and ends
#   where Jesus ends it: on the father's words, with the brother's answer
#   left open.
# - The son's confession "I have sinned against heaven and against you" is
#   kept in both places (15:18, 15:21).
# - "Wasted it all on wild living" renders 15:13; the older brother's charge
#   about prostitutes (15:30) is reduced to "wasted your money".
# - "Dead and alive again, lost and found" is the father's own phrase, twice.
# - The servant's report (15:27) is narrated rather than voiced, to keep the
#   cast to five voices.
# - Omitted: "Treat me as one of your hired servants" from the planned speech;
#   the ring and sandals are kept, the famine is kept.

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The Son Who Came Home.

NARRATOR: Jesus told this story.

JESUS: There was a man who had two sons.

NARRATOR: One day, the younger son came to his father.

SON: Father, give me my share of everything you own.

NARRATOR: [matter-of-fact] So the father gave it to him. A few days later, the younger son packed up all he had. He went away to a far country. And there he wasted it all on wild living.

NARRATOR: [softly, sad] When his money was gone, there was no food in that land. He was hungry. So he took a job feeding pigs. He was so hungry that he wanted to eat the pigs' food. But nobody gave him anything.

NARRATOR: [hopeful] Then, at last, he came to his senses.

SON: [low, ashamed] My father's workers have more bread than they can eat. And here I am, starving! I will go home to my father. I will say: Father, I have sinned against heaven, and against you. I do not deserve to be called your son any more.

NARRATOR: [determined] So he got up, and set off for home.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [joyful, building] He was still a long way off when his father saw him. His father's heart was full of love. And his father ran! He ran to his son, threw his arms around him, and kissed him.

SON: Father, I have sinned against heaven, and against you. I do not deserve to be called your son any more.

NARRATOR: But the father called to his servants.

FATHER: [overjoyed] Quick! Bring the best robe, and put it on him. Put a ring on his finger, and sandals on his feet. Let us have a feast! For this son of mine was dead, and he is alive again. He was lost, and he is found!

NARRATOR: [merry] And the party began.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [calmer] Now, the older son had been out working in the field. As he came near the house, he heard music and dancing. A servant told him that his brother was home, and that his father was giving a feast.

NARRATOR: [quietly, serious] The older brother was angry. He would not go in. So his father came out, and begged him.

BROTHER: [bitter] Look! All these years I have worked for you. I have always obeyed you. And you never gave me a party with my friends. But this son of yours wasted your money, and for him you hold a feast!

FATHER: [gently] My son, you are always with me. Everything I have is yours. But we had to be glad. Your brother was dead, and he is alive again. He was lost, and he is found.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Luke, chapter fifteen.
