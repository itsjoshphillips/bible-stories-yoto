# Fidelity notes
# - Forgiveness comes first and the healing second, as in Mark. The healing is
#   presented as the proof of Jesus's authority to forgive, which is the
#   passage's own argument (2:10).
# - The teachers of the law only think their objection (2:6-7). The narrator
#   says so before the SCRIBE voice speaks it.
# - "Son of Man" is kept as Jesus's own name for himself, explained once.
# - "Could not move his legs" explains "paralysed". Mark does not say which
#   limbs; the man is carried and later walks, so legs is a safe minimum.
# - Omitted: the place name Capernaum; "Which is easier to say" is kept.

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Down Through the Roof.

NARRATOR: [lively] Jesus was at home, and everybody heard about it. So many people came that the house was full. There was no room left. Not even outside the door! And Jesus was teaching them God's word.

NARRATOR: [gently] Then four men came along. They were carrying their friend on a mat. He could not walk. His legs would not move at all.

NARRATOR: [conspiratorial] The four men wanted to bring him to Jesus. But they could not get near, because of the crowd. So do you know what they did?

NARRATOR: [playful, building] They went up onto the roof. They made a hole in it, right above Jesus. And they lowered the mat down, down, down, with their friend lying on it.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [warmly] Jesus saw their faith. They trusted him. And he said to the man on the mat,

JESUS: [warmly] Son, your sins are forgiven.

NARRATOR: [low, secretive] Now, some teachers of the law were sitting there. They did not say anything out loud. But inside, they were thinking,

SCRIBE: [low, disapproving] Why does this man talk like that? Only God can forgive sins!

NARRATOR: Jesus knew straight away what they were thinking.

JESUS: Why are you thinking these things? Which is easier? To say, "Your sins are forgiven"? Or to say, "Get up, pick up your mat, and walk"?

JESUS: But I want you to know that the Son of Man has the right to forgive sins here on earth.

NARRATOR: [gently] The Son of Man was Jesus's name for himself. Then he turned to the man on the mat.

JESUS: [with calm authority] I say to you: get up. Pick up your mat, and go home.

[PAUSE 0.8]

NARRATOR: [joyful, amazed] And the man got up! Straight away, he picked up his mat. And he walked out, in front of them all.

NARRATOR: [excited] Everyone was amazed. They praised God, and they said,

CROWD: [astonished] We have never seen anything like this!

NARRATOR: [warm, delighted] The man's legs were well. And his sins were forgiven. Jesus can do both.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Mark, chapter two.
