# Fidelity notes
# - The passage is four verses, so this is the shortest story on the card.
#   It is lengthened only by repetition, direct address and one flagged gloss.
# - The disciples "rebuked them" (10:13) without recorded words, so the
#   narrator reports it and no line is invented for them.
# - Jesus's displeasure is kept: Mark says he was "indignant" (10:14).
# - Both sayings are kept, including the harder one: whoever does not receive
#   the kingdom of God like a child will never enter it (10:15).
# - "God's kingdom belongs to children like these" renders "to such belongs
#   the kingdom of God".
# - Interpretive gloss, flagged for parental review: "Jesus has not changed.
#   Children can still come to him today" (see Hebrews 13:8, Matthew 11:28).
# - "One, and then another" unpacks "he took them in his arms" (10:16).
# - Mark says "they were bringing children"; who brought them is not stated,
#   so the script says "people".

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Jesus and the Children.

NARRATOR: [cheerful] Everywhere Jesus went, crowds of people came to him. And one day, people began bringing their children to Jesus. They wanted Jesus to put his hands on them, and bless them.

NARRATOR: [disappointed] But Jesus's friends, the disciples, told them off. They tried to send the children away.

NARRATOR: [quietly, serious] Jesus saw it. And Jesus was not pleased with his friends. Not pleased at all.

JESUS: [firmly] Let the little children come to me. Do not stop them! God's kingdom belongs to children like these.

NARRATOR: [delighted] Did you hear that? Let the children come. Do not stop them. Jesus wanted the children close to him.

NARRATOR: Then Jesus said something more, for everyone to hear.

JESUS: I tell you the truth. Anyone who will not receive God's kingdom like a little child will never go into it.

NARRATOR: [joyful] And then the children came. Nobody stopped them now.

NARRATOR: [warm, tender] Jesus took the children in his arms. One, and then another, and then another. He put his hands on them. And he blessed them.

NARRATOR: The disciples tried to keep the children away. But Jesus said, let them come. He took them in his arms, and he blessed them.

NARRATOR: [bright, warm] And Jesus has not changed. Children can still come to him today.

NARRATOR: [softly] God's kingdom belongs to children like these.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Mark, chapter ten.
