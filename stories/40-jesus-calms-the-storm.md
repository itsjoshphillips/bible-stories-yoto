# Fidelity notes
# - Every spoken line is a close paraphrase of Mark's wording.
# - The disciples' second fear ("they were filled with great fear", 4:41) is
#   kept and explained, not dropped: it is the point of the passage.
# - Interpretive gloss, flagged for parental review: "Only God can tell the wind
#   and the sea what to do" (see Psalm 107:28-29, Psalm 89:9). Mark leaves the
#   question hanging; delete the line if you prefer to leave it hanging too.
# - Omitted: "other boats were with him" (4:36).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Jesus Calms the Storm.

NARRATOR: [calm, setting the scene] All day long, Jesus had been teaching a great crowd of people beside the lake. Now it was evening. Jesus said to his friends,

JESUS: Let us go across to the other side.

NARRATOR: [gently] So they left the crowd behind, and they set off across the water in a boat. Jesus lay down at the back of the boat, with his head on a cushion. And he fell fast asleep.

[SFX: wind rising over open water, waves slapping a wooden boat | 4]

NARRATOR: [building excitement] Then the wind began to blow. It blew harder, and harder. A great storm came roaring across the lake. The waves grew tall. They crashed against the boat. Crash! They broke right over the side. Splash! The boat was filling up with water.

NARRATOR: [wide-eyed] Jesus's friends were so afraid. And where was Jesus? [quietly] Still asleep, on the cushion.

DISCIPLE: [shouting, frightened] Teacher! Wake up! Don't you care that we are going to drown?

NARRATOR: [quiet, expectant] Jesus woke up. And he spoke to the wind, and to the waves.

JESUS: [firm, with calm authority] Peace. Be still.

[PAUSE 1.2]

NARRATOR: [softly] And the wind stopped. The waves lay down flat. Everything was calm. And quiet. And still.

NARRATOR: [gently] Then Jesus turned to his friends.

JESUS: [gently] Why are you so afraid? Do you still have no faith?

NARRATOR: [hushed, awed] His friends stared at Jesus. And now they felt a different kind of fear: the trembling, wide-eyed kind you feel when you stand close to someone very, very great. They said to one another,

DISCIPLE_2: [hushed, awed] Who is this?

DISCIPLE: [whispers] Even the wind and the waves obey him.

NARRATOR: [full of wonder] Who is he? Only God can tell the wind and the sea what to do. And Jesus did it with three little words.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Mark, chapter four.
