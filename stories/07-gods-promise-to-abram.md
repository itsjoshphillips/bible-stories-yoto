# Fidelity notes
# - The narrator says "Abram" and "Sarai", as these chapters do. The speaker
#   label is ABRAHAM so that he keeps one voice across the card.
# - God's speeches are close paraphrase of 12:1-3, 12:7, 15:1 and 15:4-5.
#   Abram's speech paraphrases 15:2-3.
# - "God did not tell Abram where the land was" rests on "the land that I will
#   show you" (12:1) and Hebrews 11:8.
# - "They had no children" comes from 11:30 and 15:2.
# - "God counted Abram as right with him" renders 15:6.
# - Omitted: "I will curse him who dishonours you" (12:3); Shechem, Bethel and
#   the second altar; Eliezer's name; the covenant ceremony (15:7-21).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: God's Promise to Abram.

NARRATOR: [bright] There was a man named Abram. Later on, God would give him a new name: Abraham. Abram lived with his wife, Sarai, in a place called Haran. And one day, the Lord spoke to him.

GOD: Abram. Leave your country. Leave your family, and your father's house. Go to the land that I will show you. I will make you into a great nation. I will bless you, and make your name great, and you will be a blessing. And through you, every family on earth will be blessed.

NARRATOR: [with wonder] God did not tell Abram where the land was. But Abram went, just as the Lord had told him. He was seventy-five years old. He took his wife Sarai, and his nephew Lot, and everything they owned. And they set out.

NARRATOR: [lively] On, and on, they travelled, until they came to the land of Canaan. Other people were living there already. But the Lord appeared to Abram, and said,

GOD: I will give this land to your children.

NARRATOR: [warmly] So Abram built an altar there, and he worshipped the Lord.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [quietly, wistful] But Abram and Sarai had no children. Not even one. Then one night, the Lord spoke to Abram again.

GOD: [gently] Do not be afraid, Abram. I am your shield. Your reward will be very great.

ABRAHAM: [troubled] Lord God, what will you give me? I still have no child. A servant in my house will get everything I own.

GOD: No. He will not. Your very own son will get it.

NARRATOR: [hushed] Then God took Abram outside, under the night sky.

GOD: Look up at the sky, and count the stars, if you can. That is how many your children will be.

NARRATOR: [slow, amazed] Abram looked up. Stars, and stars, and more stars. Far too many to count.

NARRATOR: [warm, glad] And Abram believed the Lord. He trusted that God would do what he said. And God counted Abram as right with him.

NARRATOR: [warm, sure] God had made a promise. And God always keeps his promises.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Genesis, chapters twelve and fifteen.
