# Fidelity notes
# - Harmonised from two Gospels in two scenes: the foot washing (John 13),
#   the bread and cup (Luke 22:19-20), then the garden and arrest (Luke
#   22:39-54). Spoken lines are close paraphrase of those verses.
# - Stated plainly: Jesus is betrayed by a friend, seized and led away. The
#   track ends on Jesus's own prayer, "your will be done", and leads directly
#   into story 53.
# - "Cup" in the prayer is explained as "this suffering" (interpretive gloss,
#   see Mark 10:38 and Isaiah 51:17).
# - Omitted: Peter's "not only my feet" (John 13:9-10), the sweat like drops
#   of blood and the angel (Luke 22:43-44), "This is your hour" (22:53), the
#   announcement of the betrayer at the table (22:21-23).
# - Kept, stated once without detail: a disciple cuts off the ear of the high
#   priest's servant and Jesus heals it (Luke 22:50-51).
# - The words over the bread and cup are given as Luke gives them, with no
#   explanation added, so that each family can teach them in its own
#   tradition.

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The Last Supper and the Garden.

NARRATOR: [quietly] It was evening in Jerusalem. Jesus and his disciples were in an upstairs room, eating a special supper together. Jesus knew that it was nearly time for him to die.

NARRATOR: [with wonder] During the supper, Jesus stood up. He tied a towel around his waist. He poured water into a bowl. Then he knelt down, and he began to wash his disciples' dusty feet. That was a servant's job! When he came to Peter, Peter said,

PETER: [shocked] Lord, are you going to wash my feet? No. You shall never wash my feet!

JESUS: [gently] If I do not wash you, you do not belong to me.

NARRATOR: [gently] So Jesus washed their feet, every one. Then he sat down again.

JESUS: Do you understand what I have done? You call me Teacher and Lord, and you are right. That is what I am. Now I, your Lord, have washed your feet. So you must wash one another's feet. I have shown you what to do.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [quietly, serious] Then Jesus took some bread. He thanked God for it, and he broke it, and he gave it to them.

JESUS: This is my body, which is given for you. Do this to remember me.

NARRATOR: After supper, he took a cup of wine.

JESUS: This cup is the new promise in my blood, which is poured out for you.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [hushed] Then they went out into the night, to a garden on the Mount of Olives. Jesus said to his disciples,

JESUS: Pray, so that you will not give in when you are tested.

NARRATOR: [softly] He walked a little way on, and he knelt down, and he prayed.

JESUS: [low, sorrowful] Father, if you are willing, take this cup away from me. [steady] But not what I want. Let what you want be done.

NARRATOR: [gently, serious] The cup meant all the suffering that was coming. Jesus did not run from it. When he got up from praying, he found his disciples fast asleep.

JESUS: Why are you sleeping? Get up, and pray.

NARRATOR: [low, tense] While he was still speaking, a crowd came into the garden. And leading them was Judas, one of Jesus's own twelve disciples. Judas came close to Jesus, to give him a kiss.

JESUS: [sadly] Judas, are you handing me over with a kiss?

NARRATOR: [startled] One of the disciples swung a sword, and cut off a man's ear.

JESUS: [firm] No more of this!

NARRATOR: [gently] And Jesus touched the man's ear, and healed him.

NARRATOR: [quietly, sad] Then they grabbed hold of Jesus, and they led him away into the night.

NARRATOR: [calm, sure] Jesus could have stopped them. But he had prayed, "Father, let what you want be done." And he went with them.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Luke, chapter twenty-two.
