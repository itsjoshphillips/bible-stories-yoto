# Fidelity notes
# - Harmonised: the earthquake, the angel, the guards and the angel's words
#   to the women are from Matthew 28:1-7; Mary Magdalene's meeting with Jesus
#   is from John 20:11-18. The exact order of the morning's events across the
#   Gospels is debated; this is one common arrangement.
# - Spoken lines are close paraphrase of those verses. "Rabboni" is replaced
#   by its meaning, "Teacher", which John himself supplies (20:16).
# - Simplified: Jesus's message in John 20:17 is shortened to "I am going up
#   to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God"; "Do not hold on
#   to me" is kept.
# - Omitted: Peter and the other disciple running to the tomb (John 20:2-10),
#   the two angels inside the tomb (John 20:12-13), the women meeting Jesus
#   on the road (Matthew 28:8-10).
# - "Just as he said" picks up the promise that closes story 53.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is a very happy story. It is called: Jesus Is Alive!

NARRATOR: Jesus had died on the cross. His body lay in the tomb, with a great, heavy stone across the door. Soldiers stood outside to guard it. One day went by. Then another.

NARRATOR: Then came the third day. Very early in the morning, Mary Magdalene and another woman, who had both followed Jesus, went to look at the tomb.

[SFX: deep rumble of an earthquake, stones shifting | 3]

NARRATOR: Suddenly, the ground shook! An angel of the Lord came down from heaven. He rolled the big stone away, and he sat on it. He was as bright as lightning, and his clothes were as white as snow. The soldiers shook with fear, and fell down like dead men. But the angel spoke to the women.

ANGEL: [warm, glad] Do not be afraid. I know you are looking for Jesus, who died on the cross. He is not here. He has risen, just as he said! Come, and see the place where he lay.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Mary Magdalene stood outside the tomb, and she was crying. Then she turned around. Someone was standing there. It was Jesus! But Mary did not know that it was him.

JESUS: [gently] Woman, why are you crying? Who are you looking for?

NARRATOR: Mary thought he must be the gardener.

MARY_MAGDALENE: [tearful] Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have put him, and I will go and get him.

NARRATOR: Then Jesus said just one word.

JESUS: [tenderly] Mary.

[PAUSE 1.0]

NARRATOR: And she knew! She knew his voice. She turned to him.

MARY_MAGDALENE: [joyful, astonished] Teacher!

JESUS: Do not hold on to me. Go to my brothers, and tell them this. I am going up to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.

NARRATOR: So Mary Magdalene ran to the disciples with the news.

MARY_MAGDALENE: [breathless, joyful] I have seen the Lord!

NARRATOR: The tomb was empty. Jesus was not dead any more. He was alive, just as he had promised. And he is alive today.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of John, chapter twenty.
