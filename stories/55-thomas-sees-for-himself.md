# Fidelity notes
# - Follows John closely; every spoken line is a close paraphrase.
# - The nail marks and the wound in Jesus's side are named plainly, once.
# - John says the doors were locked "for fear of the Jews", meaning the
#   authorities who had Jesus killed; the script says "the leaders".
# - The text does not say whether Thomas actually touched the wounds, so the
#   script does not say either.
# - Omitted: "Receive the Holy Spirit" and the saying about forgiving sins
#   (20:22-23); Thomas's nickname, the Twin (20:24).
# - The closing sentence applies Jesus's blessing (20:29) to the listener.
#   That application is the plain sense of the verse.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Thomas Sees for Himself.

NARRATOR: It was the evening of the day that Jesus rose from the dead. His disciples were all together in a room. The doors were locked, because they were afraid of the leaders who had killed Jesus.

NARRATOR: And then, Jesus came. The doors were still locked. But there he was, standing right in the middle of them.

JESUS: [warmly] Peace be with you.

NARRATOR: He showed them his hands, and he showed them his side. There were the marks from the cross. It was really him! The disciples were so glad to see the Lord.

JESUS: Peace be with you. As the Father sent me, so I am sending you.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: But one of the disciples was not there that night. His name was Thomas. When Thomas came back, the others all told him,

DISCIPLE: [excited] We have seen the Lord!

NARRATOR: But Thomas shook his head.

THOMAS: [stubborn] I must see the nail marks in his hands, and put my finger where the nails were. I must put my hand into his side. If I do not, I will never believe it.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Eight days went by. The disciples were in the room again. And this time, Thomas was with them. The doors were locked, just like before.

NARRATOR: And Jesus came, and stood in the middle of them.

JESUS: Peace be with you.

NARRATOR: Then Jesus turned to Thomas. He knew exactly what Thomas had said.

JESUS: [gently] Thomas. Put your finger here, and look at my hands. Reach out your hand, and put it into my side. Stop doubting, and believe.

[PAUSE 1.0]

THOMAS: [awed, quietly] My Lord, and my God!

JESUS: You believe because you have seen me. Blessed are the people who have not seen me, and still believe.

NARRATOR: Thomas saw Jesus with his own eyes, and he believed. We have not seen Jesus with our eyes. But Jesus says that everyone who believes in him is blessed. That means us, too.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of John, chapter twenty.
