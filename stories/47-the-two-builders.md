# Fidelity notes
# - The parable is the end of the Sermon on the Mount; the hillside setting
#   comes from Matthew 5:1.
# - Jesus's own comparison is kept exactly: the wise builder is "everyone who
#   hears these words of mine and does them", the foolish builder hears and
#   does not do. No other meaning is supplied.
# - Not softened: the second house falls, "and great was the fall of it".
# - The crowd's astonishment at Jesus's authority (7:28-29) closes the story,
#   as it closes the chapter. "Not like their own teachers" renders "not as
#   their scribes".
# - The narrator shares the telling of the storm for rhythm; every event in it
#   is from 7:25 and 7:27.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The Two Builders.

NARRATOR: Jesus sat down on a hillside, and great crowds of people gathered to listen. He taught them many things. And at the very end, he told them this story.

JESUS: Everyone who hears these words of mine, and does them, is like a wise man who built his house on the rock.

[SFX: heavy rain and wind against a house | 4]

NARRATOR: The rain came down. The rivers came up. The wind blew hard, and beat against that house.

JESUS: But it did not fall. It was built on the rock.

[PAUSE 0.8]

NARRATOR: Then Jesus told them about another builder.

JESUS: Everyone who hears these words of mine, and does not do them, is like a foolish man who built his house on the sand.

NARRATOR: The rain came down. The rivers came up. The wind blew hard, and beat against that house.

JESUS: [gravely] And it fell. And what a great crash it made!

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Two builders. Two houses. The very same storm.

NARRATOR: One house stood on the rock. That is like the person who hears what Jesus says, and does it.

NARRATOR: One house stood on the sand. That is like the person who hears what Jesus says, and does not do it.

NARRATOR: When Jesus had finished speaking, the crowds were amazed at his teaching. He did not teach like their own teachers. He taught like someone who was truly in charge.

NARRATOR: The words of Jesus are like rock. Hear them, and do them, and your house will stand.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Matthew, chapter seven.
