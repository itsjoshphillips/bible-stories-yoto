# Fidelity notes
# - Not softened: God's command, the binding of Isaac and the knife are all
#   stated plainly, once each. The script says at the outset that this was a
#   test, because the text says so at the outset (22:1).
# - All speech is close paraphrase of Genesis 22. "We will come back to you"
#   (22:5) is kept as Abraham said it.
# - The voice from heaven is "the angel of the LORD", who speaks as God ("you
#   have not kept back your son from me"). The speaker ANGEL_OF_THE_LORD is
#   therefore given the same voice as GOD.
# - One explanatory sentence defines a sacrifice for a small child.
# - The ending follows 22:19, which mentions Abraham returning to his servants
#   and does not mention Isaac; the script does the same.
# - Isaac's age is not given in the text. He is "the boy" and carries the wood.
# - Omitted: "possess the gate of their enemies" (22:17); Beersheba.

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Abraham and Isaac on the Mountain.

NARRATOR: [warm, tender] Isaac was the son God had promised, the son Abraham and Sarah had waited for, for so many years. And Abraham loved him.

NARRATOR: [gently, serious] Some time later, God tested Abraham.

GOD: Abraham!

ABRAHAM: Here I am.

GOD: Take your son, your only son, Isaac, whom you love. Go to the land of Moriah. And there, on a mountain I will show you, offer him to me as a sacrifice.

NARRATOR: [quietly, serious] A sacrifice was a gift for God, that was killed and burned on an altar. It was usually a lamb. But God had said: Isaac.

NARRATOR: [quietly] Early the next morning, Abraham got up. He cut wood for the fire, and he loaded his donkey. He took two servants, and his son Isaac, and they set out. On the third day, Abraham looked up, and saw the place far away.

ABRAHAM: Stay here with the donkey. The boy and I will go over there and worship. And then we will come back to you.

NARRATOR: [slow, quiet] Abraham gave Isaac the wood to carry. He himself carried the fire, and the knife. And the two of them walked on together.

ISAAC: Father?

ABRAHAM: Yes, my son?

ISAAC: We have the fire, and the wood. But where is the lamb for the sacrifice?

ABRAHAM: [quiet, steady] God himself will provide the lamb, my son.

NARRATOR: [slow, quiet] And the two of them walked on together.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [hushed, serious] When they came to the place, Abraham built an altar, and laid the wood on it. Then he tied up his son Isaac, and laid him on the altar, on top of the wood. And Abraham reached out his hand, and took the knife to kill his son.

NARRATOR: [sudden, urgent] But the angel of the Lord called to him from heaven.

ANGEL_OF_THE_LORD: [urgent] Abraham! Abraham!

ABRAHAM: Here I am!

ANGEL_OF_THE_LORD: Do not lay a hand on the boy. Do not do anything to him. Now I know that you fear God, because you have not kept back your son, your only son, from me.

NARRATOR: [relieved, amazed] Abraham looked up. And there, behind him, was a ram, a big male sheep, caught by its horns in a bush. Abraham went and took the ram. And he offered it as the sacrifice, instead of his son.

NARRATOR: [warmly] Abraham gave that place a name. He called it: The Lord Will Provide.

NARRATOR: Then the angel of the Lord called from heaven a second time.

ANGEL_OF_THE_LORD: [warmly] Because you have done this, I will surely bless you. Your children's children will be as many as the stars in the sky, and the sand on the seashore. And through your family, all the nations of the earth will be blessed, because you obeyed my voice.

NARRATOR: [gently] Then Abraham went back to his servants, and they set off for home.

NARRATOR: [warm, glad] God did not take Abraham's son. God himself provided the sacrifice.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Genesis, chapter twenty-two.
