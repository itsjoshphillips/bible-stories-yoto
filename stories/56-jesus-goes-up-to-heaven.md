# Fidelity notes
# - Two scenes from two books: the mountain in Galilee (Matthew 28:16-20) and
#   the ascension near Jerusalem (Acts 1:3-11). Spoken lines are close
#   paraphrase of those verses.
# - "Some doubted" (Matthew 28:17) is kept as "some were still not sure".
# - Acts calls the messengers "two men in white robes"; the script uses the
#   same words and does not call them angels. They are voiced by the ANGEL
#   slot only as a casting convenience.
# - Simplified: "Jerusalem, all Judea and Samaria, and the end of the earth"
#   (Acts 1:8) becomes "in Jerusalem, and in all the country round about, and
#   to the very ends of the earth".
# - Omitted: the disciples' question about the kingdom (Acts 1:6-7) and
#   "John baptised with water" (Acts 1:5).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Jesus Goes Up to Heaven.

NARRATOR: [joyful] Jesus was alive again! For forty days, his disciples saw him, again and again. He talked with them about God's kingdom.

NARRATOR: [setting the scene] One day, the eleven disciples went to a mountain in Galilee, where Jesus had told them to go. When they saw him, they worshipped him. But some were still not sure. Jesus came close, and he said,

JESUS: [calm, strong] All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. So go, and make disciples of all nations. Baptise them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Teach them to do everything that I have told you. [warmly] And remember this. I am with you always, to the very end.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [leaning in] Later, Jesus was with them near Jerusalem. He told them not to leave the city yet. They must wait there for the gift that God the Father had promised.

JESUS: In a few days, you will be baptised with the Holy Spirit. You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you. And you will tell people about me: in Jerusalem, and in all the country round about, and to the very ends of the earth.

NARRATOR: [slow, amazed] When Jesus had said this, something wonderful happened. As they were watching, he was lifted up. Up, and up, and up. Then a cloud hid him, and they could not see him any more.

[PAUSE 1.2]

NARRATOR: [surprised] The disciples stood there, staring up into the sky. And suddenly, two men in white robes were standing beside them.

ANGEL: [kindly] Men of Galilee, why are you standing here, looking up into the sky? This Jesus, who has been taken up from you into heaven, will come back. He will come in the same way that you saw him go.

NARRATOR: [warm, sure] Jesus had gone up to heaven, to be with God his Father. He is the King, with all authority in heaven and on earth.

NARRATOR: [gladly] And his friends still had his two great promises to hold on to. "I am with you always." And, one day, he will come back.

NARRATOR: [warmly] You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Acts, chapter one.
