# Fidelity notes
# - Speech is close paraphrase of Matthew 2:2, 5-6, 8, 13.
# - Nothing is added from tradition: Matthew does not say there were three
#   wise men, does not call them kings, and has them come to a house and a
#   child, not to the manger (2:11).
# - Not softened: Herod's lie and his plan to kill the child are stated
#   plainly (2:8, 13).
# - Flagged for parental review, omitted: Herod's killing of the baby boys of
#   Bethlehem (2:16-18) falls just outside this story's reference and is left
#   out for age.
# - The priests' answer quotes Micah 5:2, which the listener heard in story 33.
# - Omitted: "all Jerusalem with him" was troubled (2:3); the prophecy "Out of
#   Egypt I called my son" (2:15).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: The Wise Men and the Star.

NARRATOR: [with wonder] After Jesus was born in Bethlehem, some visitors came to the great city of Jerusalem. They were wise men, from a land far away in the east. And they were looking for someone.

WISE_MAN: Where is the child who has been born king of the Jews? We saw his star when it rose, and we have come to worship him.

NARRATOR: [low, uneasy] Now, there was already a king in Jerusalem. His name was Herod. And when King Herod heard about a new king, he was very troubled. He called together the priests and the teachers of God's law.

HEROD: [sharp] Where is God's promised King to be born?

PRIEST: In Bethlehem. That is what the prophet wrote, long ago. "Out of you, Bethlehem, will come a ruler for my people Israel."

NARRATOR: [hushed, secretive] Then Herod called the wise men to him, in secret. He asked them just when the star had appeared. And he sent them off to Bethlehem.

HEROD: [smooth, false] Go and search carefully for the child. When you have found him, come back and tell me. Then I can go and worship him too.

NARRATOR: [low, serious] But that was a lie. Herod did not want to worship the child at all.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [excited, joyful] The wise men set off. And look! There was the star they had seen in the east. It went ahead of them, until it stopped over the place where the child was. When they saw the star, they were filled with joy. Great, great joy!

NARRATOR: [softly, awed] They went into the house. And there they saw the child, with Mary his mother. The wise men fell down on their knees, and they worshipped him. Then they opened their treasures, and gave him presents: gold, and sweet-smelling frankincense, and myrrh.

NARRATOR: [hushed] That night, God warned the wise men in a dream. They must not go back to Herod. So they went home by another road.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [quietly] When they had gone, an angel of the Lord came to Joseph in a dream.

ANGEL: [urgent] Get up! Take the child and his mother, and run away to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you. Herod is going to look for the child, to kill him.

NARRATOR: [hushed, urgent] Joseph got up at once. In the dark of the night, he took the child and his mother, and they set out for Egypt. And there they stayed, safe and sound, until King Herod had died.

NARRATOR: [warm, sure] Wise men came from far away to worship Jesus. A wicked king tried to stop him. But nobody can stop God's promised King.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Matthew, chapter two.
