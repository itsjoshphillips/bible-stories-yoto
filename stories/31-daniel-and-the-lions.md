# Fidelity notes
# - Speech is close paraphrase of Daniel 6:5, 7, 16, 20-22, 26-27.
# - Not softened: the plot, the den, and the fate of the plotters (6:24),
#   stated once.
# - Omitted as too heavy: the plotters' wives and children were thrown in with
#   them, and the lions broke their bones (6:24).
# - "Just as he had always done": 6:10.
# - Omitted for length: the 120 satraps and three presidents (6:1-2) and the
#   sealing of the stone with signet rings (6:17).

NARRATOR: [warm, bright, lively storyteller speaking to small children] This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Daniel and the Lions.

NARRATOR: [brightly] Daniel lived in Babylon, far from his home. He worked for the king, King Darius. Daniel did his work so well that the king wanted to put him in charge of the whole kingdom.

NARRATOR: [low, sly] The other leaders did not like that. They looked for something Daniel had done wrong. But they could find nothing. Not one thing.

PLOTTER: [scheming] We will never catch this Daniel out, unless it has to do with the law of his God.

NARRATOR: [hushed] So they made a plan. They went to the king.

PLOTTER: [smoothly] O king, make a new law. For thirty days, nobody may pray to any god or any man, except to you. Anyone who does will be thrown into the lions' den.

NARRATOR: [serious] King Darius signed the law. And once a law was signed, it could never be changed.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: [warm, steady] Daniel heard about the new law. And what did he do? He went home. He went upstairs, where the windows were open towards Jerusalem. He got down on his knees. And he prayed, and gave thanks to his God. Three times a day, just as he had always done.

NARRATOR: [hurried] The men were watching. They ran to tell the king.

NARRATOR: [worried] The king was very upset. All day long, until the sun went down, he tried to find a way to save Daniel. But the law could not be changed. So Daniel was brought out, and thrown into the den of lions.

DARIUS: [anxious] May your God, whom you always serve, rescue you!

NARRATOR: [quietly, tense] A great stone was laid across the opening. The king went home to his palace. He would not eat. And all night long, he could not sleep.

[PAUSE 1.2]

NARRATOR: [hurried, anxious] At the first light of morning, the king got up and hurried to the lions' den.

DARIUS: [calling, afraid] Daniel! Servant of the living God! Has your God been able to rescue you from the lions?

DANIEL: [calm, warm] O king, live for ever! My God sent his angel, and he shut the lions' mouths. They have not hurt me.

NARRATOR: [joyful, relieved] The king was overjoyed. Daniel was lifted up out of the den. There was not a scratch on him, because he had trusted in his God.

NARRATOR: [plainly, serious] Then the king gave an order. The men who had set the trap for Daniel were thrown to the lions themselves. And King Darius wrote to all the people in his kingdom.

DARIUS: [proclaiming] Everyone must honour the God of Daniel. He is the living God, and he lasts for ever. He rescues and he saves. He has saved Daniel from the power of the lions.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Daniel, chapter six.
