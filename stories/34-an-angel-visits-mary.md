# Fidelity notes
# - Speech is close paraphrase of Luke 1:28-38, 46-49 and Matthew 1:20-21.
# - Mary's question, "since I am a virgin" (Luke 1:34), is given as "I am not
#   married yet", which keeps the sense for small children. The angel's answer
#   (1:35) is kept in full: the baby comes by the Holy Spirit, with no human
#   father.
# - Joseph "decided he should not marry her" stands for "resolved to divorce
#   her quietly" (Matthew 1:19).
# - Mary's song (Luke 1:46-55) is reduced to its opening lines.
# - Omitted for length: Elizabeth's words and the baby leaping (1:41-45);
#   Elizabeth's pregnancy is kept as the angel's sign (1:36).
# - Two Gospels are joined: Mary's visit from Luke, Joseph's dream from Matthew.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: An Angel Visits Mary.

NARRATOR: In a little town called Nazareth, there lived a young woman named Mary. She was promised to be married to a man named Joseph. Joseph came from the family of King David, from long ago.

NARRATOR: One day, God sent an angel to Mary. The angel's name was Gabriel.

ANGEL: [warmly] Greetings, Mary! God has been very kind to you. The Lord is with you.

NARRATOR: Mary was troubled. What could this mean?

ANGEL: [gently] Do not be afraid, Mary. God is pleased with you. You are going to have a baby boy, and you must name him Jesus. He will be great. He will be called the Son of the Most High. God will give him the throne of King David. And his kingdom will never end.

MARY: [wondering] How can this be? I am not married yet.

ANGEL: The Holy Spirit will come to you, and the power of God Most High will cover you. So the holy child will be called the Son of God. And listen: your cousin Elizabeth is going to have a baby too, even though she is old. For nothing is impossible with God.

MARY: [quietly, sure] I am the Lord's servant. Let it happen to me, just as you have said.

NARRATOR: And the angel left her.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Mary hurried away to the hills, to visit Elizabeth. And there, Mary was so full of joy that she sang a song to God.

MARY: [joyful] My soul praises the Lord! My spirit is glad because of God, my Saviour. The Mighty One has done great things for me. Holy is his name!

NARRATOR: Mary stayed with Elizabeth for about three months. Then she went home again.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Now, Joseph found out that Mary was going to have a baby. He knew the baby was not his. Joseph was a good man, and he did not want to shame her. But he decided that he should not marry her after all.

NARRATOR: Then, one night, while Joseph was asleep, an angel of the Lord came to him in a dream.

ANGEL: Joseph, son of David. Do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife. The baby inside her is from the Holy Spirit. She will have a son, and you must name him Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins.

NARRATOR: Joseph woke up. And he did just what the angel had told him. He took Mary home to be his wife.

NARRATOR: God had promised a King who would rescue his people. Now the King was coming. And his name would be Jesus.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Luke, chapter one.
