# Fidelity notes
# - Spoken lines are close paraphrase of 16:1, 16:6-7, 16:10-12.
# - Background from outside the passage: Saul as Israel's first king and his
#   disobedience (1 Samuel 15), which is why God says "I have rejected him".
# - David's looks ("fine eyes", healthy and handsome) are from 16:12.
# - The Spirit of the Lord coming on David (16:13) is kept.
# - David does not become king for many years (2 Samuel 2:4, 5:3); the
#   script says so, to avoid implying he reigned at once.
# - Omitted for length: Samuel's fear of Saul and the sacrifice (16:2-5),
#   the names Abinadab and Shammah.

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: David the Shepherd King.

NARRATOR: God's people had a king now. His name was King Saul. But Saul did not obey God. So God spoke to Samuel, who was an old man by this time.

GOD: I have rejected Saul as king. Fill your horn with oil, and go to Bethlehem, to a man named Jesse. I have chosen one of his sons to be the new king.

NARRATOR: So Samuel went to Bethlehem. Jesse came to meet him, and he brought his sons.

NARRATOR: The oldest son was called Eliab. He was tall, and strong, and handsome. Samuel took one look at him, and he thought,

SAMUEL: [to himself, pleased] Surely this is the one the Lord has chosen.

NARRATOR: But God said to Samuel,

GOD: [calm, warm] Do not look at his face, or at how tall he is. I have not chosen him. The Lord does not see as people see. People look at the outside. But the Lord looks at the heart.

NARRATOR: Then Jesse called his next son. But Samuel shook his head. The Lord had not chosen him. And the next son. Not him either.

NARRATOR: Seven sons walked past Samuel. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven. And every time, the answer was no.

SAMUEL: The Lord has not chosen any of these. Are these all the sons you have?

JESSE: There is still the youngest one. But he is out looking after the sheep.

SAMUEL: Send for him. We will not sit down until he comes.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: So they ran and fetched the youngest son, in from the fields. His name was David. He was healthy and handsome, and he had fine eyes. And God said,

GOD: Get up, and anoint him. This is the one.

NARRATOR: Samuel took his horn of oil. He poured the oil on David's head, in front of all his big brothers. That was the sign that God had chosen David to be king. And from that day on, the Spirit of the Lord came upon David with power.

NARRATOR: David did not become king straight away. That came years later. For now, he went back to his sheep.

NARRATOR: His father had not even called him in from the fields. But God had seen David all along. For people look at the outside. But the Lord looks at the heart.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the first book of Samuel, chapter sixteen.
