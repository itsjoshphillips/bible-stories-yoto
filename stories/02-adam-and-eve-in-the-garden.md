# Fidelity notes
# - The man is called Adam from the start for clarity; Genesis 2 mostly says
#   "the man". The woman is not named in this story because she is not named
#   Eve until Genesis 3:20; the title uses the familiar name.
# - God's two speeches and Adam's speech are close paraphrase of 2:16-18, 23.
# - "One flesh" (2:24) is rendered "the two become one".
# - Small explanatory gloss: "They had nothing to hide" explains "not ashamed"
#   (2:25). Delete if you prefer the bare statement.
# - Omitted: the four named rivers, the gold and onyx (2:10-14), the mist (2:6).

NARRATOR: This is a story from God's book, the Bible. It is called: Adam and Eve in the Garden.

NARRATOR: When God made the world, he made one thing in a very special way. God took dust from the ground, and he shaped it into a man. Then God breathed into the man the breath of life. And the man came alive! His name was Adam.

NARRATOR: God planted a garden for Adam, in a place called Eden. God made trees grow there: trees that were lovely to look at, with fruit that was good to eat. A river flowed through the garden to water it. And in the middle of the garden stood two special trees. One was the tree of life. The other was the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.

NARRATOR: God put Adam in the garden to work in it, and to take care of it. And God gave Adam one rule.

GOD: [calm, clear] You may eat from every tree in the garden. But you must not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. If you eat from that tree, you will surely die.

[SCENE]

NARRATOR: Then God said,

GOD: It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper who is just right for him.

NARRATOR: God had made all the animals, and all the birds, out of the ground. Now he brought them to Adam, to see what Adam would call them. One by one they came. And whatever Adam called each one, that was its name.

NARRATOR: Adam gave names to the cattle. He gave names to the birds. He gave names to every wild animal. But not one of them was the right helper for Adam.

NARRATOR: So God made Adam fall into a deep, deep sleep. While Adam slept, God took one of his ribs, and closed up the place where it had been. And from the rib, God made a woman. Then God brought her to Adam.

ADAM: [with joy] At last! Bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh! She will be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.

NARRATOR: That is why a man leaves his father and his mother, and holds on tight to his wife, and the two become one.

NARRATOR: Adam and his wife had no clothes on, and they were not ashamed. They had nothing to hide.

NARRATOR: God made the man, and God made the woman. He gave them a garden for their home, good work to do, and each other.

NARRATOR: You can find this story in the Bible, in the book of Genesis, chapter two.
